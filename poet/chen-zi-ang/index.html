陈子昂
Chen Zi'ang
唐 · Tang · 661–702 · 1 poem

Early Tang reformer who called poets back from court prettiness to the strength of the Han and Wei. His four lines on Youzhou Terrace are one of the loneliest cries in Chinese verse.
