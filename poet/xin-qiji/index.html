辛弃疾
Xin Qiji
宋 · Song · 1140–1207 · 10 poems

Born under Jurchen rule, he led a daring raid and fled south at 22 to serve the Song, but was given no army and spent decades in retirement. The greatest heroic ci poet, paired with Su Shi.
