王维
Wang Wei
唐 · Tang · 701–761 · 17 poems

Poet, painter, musician and devout Buddhist — "in his poems there are paintings, in his paintings poems". Master of quiet landscape verse written at his Wangchuan estate. Called the "Poet Buddha".
