李白
Li Bai
唐 · Tang · 701–762 · 34 poems

Also Li Po. The "Immortal of Poetry": a free spirit who wandered the empire with sword and wine jug, served briefly at Emperor Xuanzong's court, and wrote with dazzling spontaneity of the moon, mountains, friendship and wine. With Du Fu, the greatest of Chinese poets.
