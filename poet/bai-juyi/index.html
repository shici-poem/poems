白居易
Bai Juyi
唐 · Tang · 772–846 · 13 poems

Also Po Chü-i. Believed poems should be understood by everyone — he read drafts to an old woman and rewrote what she could not follow. Author of The Song of Everlasting Sorrow and The Pipa Player, and of satires on the people's suffering.
