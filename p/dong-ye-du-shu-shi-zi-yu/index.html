冬夜读书示子聿

〔宋〕陆游

古人学问无遗力，少壮工夫老始成。

纸上得来终觉浅，绝知此事要躬行。

英译 English translation

Reading on a Winter Night: To My Son Yu

Dōng Yè Dú Shū Shì Zǐ Yù
Lu You · Song · Seven-character quatrain

The ancients spared no effort in their studies, young or old;

What they began in youth was only finished when they aged.

What's learned from books will always seem a shallow hold —

To truly know a thing, in doing it you must engage.

About this poem

Advice to his youngest son on a winter night of reading. 纸上得来终觉浅，绝知此事要躬行 — "knowledge from paper is always shallow; to truly know, you must do it yourself" — is one of the most quoted maxims on learning in Chinese.
