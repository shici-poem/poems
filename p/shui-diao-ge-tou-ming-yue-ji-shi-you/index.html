水调歌头（明月几时有）

〔宋〕苏轼

明月几时有？把酒问青天。

不知天上宫阙，今夕是何年。

我欲乘风归去，又恐琼楼玉宇，高处不胜寒。

起舞弄清影，何似在人间。

转朱阁，低绮户，照无眠。

不应有恨，何事长向别时圆？

人有悲欢离合，月有阴晴圆缺，此事古难全。

但愿人长久，千里共婵娟。

英译 English translation

Prelude to the Water Melody (When Did the Bright Moon Appear)

Shuǐ Diào Gē Tóu Míng Yuè Jǐ Shí Yǒu
Su Shi · Song · Ci lyric

How long has the bright moon been there?

Wine cup in hand, I ask the azure sky.

I wonder, in the palaces of heaven,

What year it is tonight, on high.

I'd like to ride the wind and fly back home,

But fear those crystal towers and halls of jade

Would be too cold for me, so high.

I rise and dance with my clear shadow here —

Who'd trade the human world for heaven? Not I!

It rounds the red pavilion,

Stoops through the silken window low,

And shines upon the sleepless.

It should bear us no ill will —

Why is it always full when we're apart? I'd like to know.

Men have their griefs and joys, their partings and reunions;

The moon has dark and bright, it waxes and it wanes —

Nothing's been perfect since long ago.

I only wish that we may both live long,

And, a thousand li apart, share the moon's lovely glow.

About this poem

Written at the Mid-Autumn Festival of 1076 in Mizhou, "drunk all night until dawn, thinking of Ziyou" — his brother Su Zhe, whom he had not seen for seven years. Su Shi dreams of flying up to the palaces of the moon but chooses the human world; then he forgives the moon for being full while families are apart, because nothing in life is ever whole. The closing wish — 但愿人长久，千里共婵娟 — is the Mid-Autumn blessing of every Chinese family, and the lyric, set to music in 1983, is known to everyone.
