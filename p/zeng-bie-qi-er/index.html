赠别（其二）

〔唐〕杜牧

多情却似总无情，唯觉樽前笑不成。

蜡烛有心还惜别，替人垂泪到天明。

英译 English translation

Parting Gift (II)

Zèng Bié Qí Èr
Du Mu · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

So full of love, I seem to have no love at all;

Over our cups I only find I cannot smile.

The candle has a heart, and grieves to see us part —

It sheds its tears for us till dawn, all the while.

About this poem

Too much feeling makes the lovers seem cold and silent. The candle's "heart" is its wick (心 means both), and its "tears" the dripping wax — so the candle weeps for the lovers who cannot.

Earlier translations 旧译

“The Last Night”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

Old love would seem as though not love today;

Spell-bound by thee, my laughter dies away.

The very wax sheds sympathetic tears

And gutters sadly down till dawn appears.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
