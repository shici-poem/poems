峨眉山月歌

〔唐〕李白

峨眉山月半轮秋，影入平羌江水流。

夜发清溪向三峡，思君不见下渝州。

英译 English translation

Song of the Moon over Mount Emei

É Méi Shān Yuè Gē
Li Bai · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

Over Mount Emei hangs the autumn half-moon's light;

Its image floats along the Pingqiang's flowing stream.

I leave Clear Creek for the Three Gorges in the night;

I miss you — you are lost to sight — and drift to Yuzhou in a dream.

About this poem

Written around 725 when the young Li Bai first left his home province of Shu. In four lines he names five places — Emei, the Pingqiang River, Clear Creek, the Three Gorges, Yuzhou — yet the poem reads as one smooth glide downstream. The "you" he misses may be the moon itself, hidden now behind the gorge cliffs, or a friend left behind.
