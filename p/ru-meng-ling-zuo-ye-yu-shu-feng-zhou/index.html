如梦令（昨夜雨疏风骤）

〔宋〕李清照

昨夜雨疏风骤，浓睡不消残酒。

试问卷帘人，却道海棠依旧。

知否，知否？应是绿肥红瘦。

英译 English translation

As in a Dream (Last Night, Scattered Rain)

Rú Mèng Lìng Zuó Yè Yǔ Shū Fēng Zhòu
Li Qingzhao · Song · Ci lyric

Last night, scattered rain and a gusty wind;

Deep sleep has not dispelled the last of the wine.

I ask the maid who's rolling up the blind —

"The crabapple's just as it was," she says, "it's fine."

"Don't you know? Don't you know?

The green must be fuller now, and the red grown thin."

About this poem

Written by Li Qingzhao as a young woman. A dialogue in miniature: the careless maid says the flowers are fine; the poet, who lay awake listening to the storm, knows better. 绿肥红瘦, "the green grows plump, the red grows thin" — leaves flourishing, blossoms stripped — was admired as a daring and perfect phrase.
