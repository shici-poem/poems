春宵

〔宋〕苏轼

春宵一刻值千金，花有清香月有阴。

歌管楼台声细细，秋千院落夜沉沉。

英译 English translation

Spring Night

Chūn Xiāo
Su Shi · Song · Seven-character quatrain

A moment of a spring night is worth a thousand gold;

The flowers breathe pure fragrance; the moon lays down its shade.

From towers and terraces, faint songs and pipes float soft;

The courtyard with its swing lies deep in night's dark glade.

About this poem

春宵一刻值千金, "a moment of a spring night is worth a thousand gold", is one of the most quoted lines in Chinese — a reminder not to waste precious time. The last two lines contrast revellers still singing in their towers with the quiet courtyards already asleep.

Earlier translations 旧译

“Spring Nights”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

One half-hour of a night in spring is worth a thousand taels,

When the clear sweet scent of flowers is felt and the moon her lustre pales;

When mellowed sounds of song and flute are borne along the breeze,

And through the stilly scene the swing sounds swishing from the trees.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
