春日忆李白

〔唐〕杜甫

白也诗无敌，飘然思不群。

清新庾开府，俊逸鲍参军。

渭北春天树，江东日暮云。

何时一樽酒，重与细论文。

英译 English translation

Remembering Li Bai on a Spring Day

Chūn Rì Yì Lǐ Bái
Du Fu · Tang · Five-character regulated verse

Li Bai — your poems have no peer;

Your thoughts float free, far from the crowd's ways:

Fresh and clear as Lord Yu Xin's,

Bold and graceful as Bao Zhao's lays.

North of the Wei, I see spring trees;

East of the River, you watch evening clouds.

When shall we share a jug of wine again,

And talk of poetry, line by line, aloud?

About this poem

Du Fu met Li Bai in 744 and travelled with him; after 745 they never met again. Du Fu wrote some fifteen poems about Li Bai. Yu Xin and Bao Zhao were great poets of the Southern Dynasties. "Spring trees north of the Wei, dusk clouds east of the River" — Du Fu in Chang'an, Li Bai in the south — became a set phrase for friends who miss each other.
