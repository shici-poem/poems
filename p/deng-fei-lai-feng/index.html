登飞来峰

〔宋〕王安石

飞来山上千寻塔，闻说鸡鸣见日升。

不畏浮云遮望眼，自缘身在最高层。

英译 English translation

Climbing Feilai Peak

Dēng Fēi Lái Fēng
Wang Anshi · Song · Seven-character quatrain

On Flying Peak there stands a pagoda, a thousand fathoms high;

They say at cockcrow you can see the sun come up from there.

I do not fear the floating clouds that block the eye,

Because I'm standing on the highest stair.

About this poem

Written in 1050 by the young Wang Anshi, full of confidence. "Floating clouds" were the standard image for slanderers who obscure the truth; stand high enough, and you will not be deceived. A favourite poem of vision and ambition.
