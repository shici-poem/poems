武陵春（风住尘香花已尽）

〔宋〕李清照

风住尘香花已尽，日晚倦梳头。

物是人非事事休，欲语泪先流。

闻说双溪春尚好，也拟泛轻舟。

只恐双溪舴艋舟，载不动许多愁。

英译 English translation

Spring at Wuling (The Wind Has Stopped)

Wǔ Líng Chūn Fēng Zhù Chén Xiāng Huā Yǐ Jìn
Li Qingzhao · Song · Ci lyric

The wind has stopped; the dust is fragrant; all the flowers have gone;

The day is late — I'm too weary to comb my hair.

Things remain, but he does not; all is ended, everyone;

I try to speak — but first the tears are there.

They say at Twin Streams spring is lovely still;

I thought I'd take a little boat out, too.

But I fear the grasshopper skiffs at Twin Streams

Could never carry all the grief I'm going through.

About this poem

Jinhua, 1135. Li Qingzhao, in her fifties, widowed and a refugee, cannot even bring herself to go boating at the famous Twin Streams. 物是人非, "things remain, but people are gone", is now a common phrase; grief too heavy for a little boat to carry is one of her most famous images.
