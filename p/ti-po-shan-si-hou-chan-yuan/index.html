题破山寺后禅院

〔唐〕常建

清晨入古寺，初日照高林。

曲径通幽处，禅房花木深。

山光悦鸟性，潭影空人心。

万籁此都寂，但余钟磬音。

英译 English translation

The Meditation Hall Behind Poshan Temple

Tí Pò Shān Sì Hòu Chán Yuàn
Chang Jian · Tang · Five-character regulated verse

At early dawn I enter the old temple's gate;

The first rays of the sun light up the forest tall.

A winding path leads to a secret, hidden place,

Where flowers and trees grow deep round the meditation hall.

The mountain light delights the nature of the birds;

The pool's reflections empty out the hearts of men.

Here all the sounds of the world fall silent,

Save for the bell and the stone chime, now and then.

About this poem

Poshan Temple is in Changshu, Jiangsu. 曲径通幽处, "a winding path leads to a secluded place", became a proverb and a principle of Chinese garden design. The poem moves from the outer landscape to inner stillness — the reflections in the pool empty the mind of desire — and ends with nothing but the temple bells.

Earlier translations 旧译

“Dhyana”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

The clear dawn creeps into the convent old,

The rising sun tips its tall trees with gold,—

As, darkly, by a winding path I reach

Dhyana's hall, hidden midst fir and beech.

Around these hills sweet birds their pleasure take,

Man's heart as free from shadows as this lake;

Here worldly sounds are hushed, as by a spell,

Save for the booming of the altar bell.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.

“The Hall of Silence”W. J. B. Fletcher, 1919

Where the sun's eye first

Peers above the pines,

On the ancient temple

Early daylight shines.

To retirement guiding

Leads the winding way:

Round the Cell of Silence

Flowers and Foliage stray.

Hark! the birds rejoicing

In the mountain light!

Like one's dim reflection

On a pool at night

Lo! the heart is melted

Wav'ring out of sight.

All is hushed to silence.

Harmony is still.

The bell's low chime alone

Whispers round the hill.

Translated by W. J. B. Fletcher, from Gems of Chinese Verse (1919) · public domain.
