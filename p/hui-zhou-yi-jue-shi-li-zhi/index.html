惠州一绝（食荔枝）

〔宋〕苏轼

罗浮山下四时春，卢橘杨梅次第新。

日啖荔枝三百颗，不辞长作岭南人。

英译 English translation

A Quatrain from Huizhou (Eating Lychees)

Huì Zhōu Yī Jué Shí Lì Zhī
Su Shi · Song · Seven-character quatrain

Below Mount Luofu it is spring all the year;

Loquats and bayberries ripen fresh, each in its turn.

With three hundred lychees to eat each day, right here,

I'd gladly live in the south forever — and never return.

About this poem

Exiled at sixty to Huizhou in Guangdong — then a malarial frontier — Su Shi wrote not a lament but a love song to lychees. His cheerful refusal to be miserable is why he is the best-loved figure in Chinese literature.
