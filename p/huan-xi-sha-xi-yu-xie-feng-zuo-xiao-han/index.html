浣溪沙（细雨斜风作晓寒）

〔宋〕苏轼

细雨斜风作晓寒，淡烟疏柳媚晴滩。入淮清洛渐漫漫。

雪沫乳花浮午盏，蓼茸蒿笋试春盘。人间有味是清欢。

英译 English translation

Washing Silk in the Stream (Fine Rain, Slanting Wind)

Huàn Xī Shā Xì Yǔ Xié Fēng Zuò Xiǎo Hán
Su Shi · Song · Ci lyric

Fine rain and slanting wind make the morning cold;

Faint mist and sparse willows grace the sunny shoal;

The clear Luo widens, wide and slow, as into the Huai it's rolled.

Snow-white froth and milky foam float in my noonday tea;

Smartweed shoots and wormwood sprouts — the spring dish, fresh and whole.

Of all life's flavours, simple joy is finest — this I hold.

About this poem

A winter outing near Sizhou in 1084, with frothy whisked tea and a "spring platter" of fresh greens. The last line — 人间有味是清欢, "the true taste of life is simple joy" — has become one of Su Shi's most loved sayings.
