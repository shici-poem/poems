逢雪宿芙蓉山主人

〔唐〕刘长卿

日暮苍山远，天寒白屋贫。

柴门闻犬吠，风雪夜归人。

英译 English translation

Lodging with the Master of Lotus Mountain in Snow

Féng Xuě Sù Fú Róng Shān Zhǔ Rén
Liu Changqing · Tang · Five-character quatrain

At sunset, the grey-blue mountains far away;

The sky is cold; the thatched hut, white and poor.

A dog barks at the brushwood gate — someone, late,

Comes home through wind and snow, at night, to the door.

About this poem

Twenty characters, a complete short film: a traveller finds shelter at dusk in a poor mountain hut; in the night a dog barks — the master is coming home through the storm. 风雪夜归人, "a man coming home on a night of wind and snow", is one of the best-loved images in Chinese.

Earlier translations 旧译

“A Winter Scene”W. J. B. Fletcher, 1919

The daylight far is fading across the purple hill.

And white the houses of the poor with winter's breathing chill.

The house dog's sudden barking, which hears the wicket go,

Greets us at night returning through driving gale and snow.

Translated by W. J. B. Fletcher, from Gems of Chinese Verse (1919) · public domain. Lightly adapted: line 1: "dawning" changed to "fading"; the Chinese is set at dusk.
