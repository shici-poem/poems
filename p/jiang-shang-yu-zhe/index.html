江上渔者

〔宋〕范仲淹

江上往来人，但爱鲈鱼美。

君看一叶舟，出没风波里。

英译 English translation

The Fisherman on the River

Jiāng Shàng Yú Zhě
Fan Zhongyan · Song · Five-character quatrain

The people strolling by the river, to and fro,

Love only the taste of perch — how sweet!

But look at that leaf of a boat out there,

Tossed in and out of sight where the wind and waves meet.

About this poem

The diners on the bank enjoy the perch; few think of the fisherman risking his life in the storm to catch it. A tiny poem of social conscience by a statesman famous for putting the people first.
