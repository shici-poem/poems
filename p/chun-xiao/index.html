春晓

〔唐〕孟浩然

春眠不觉晓，处处闻啼鸟。

夜来风雨声，花落知多少。

英译 English translation

Spring Morning

Chūn Xiǎo
Meng Haoran · Tang · Five-character quatrain

In spring I sleep and miss the break of day;

Everywhere I hear the birds sing on.

Last night, the sound of wind and rain —

How many blossoms fell before the dawn?

About this poem

After Quiet Night Thoughts, perhaps the most famous poem in Chinese. Half awake on a spring morning, the poet hears birdsong and remembers the night's storm: a moment of pure contentment shadowed by the thought of fallen petals — beauty and its passing in twenty characters.

Earlier translations 旧译

“Springtide Dreams”W. J. B. Fletcher, 1919

In Springtide's dreams the dawn is sweetly drowned;

Till everywhere the songs of birds resound.

I heard last night the rush of wind and rain.

How many Flowers have fallen to the Ground?

Translated by W. J. B. Fletcher, from Gems of Chinese Verse (1919) · public domain.
