清明

〔唐〕杜牧

清明时节雨纷纷，路上行人欲断魂。

借问酒家何处有？牧童遥指杏花村。

英译 English translation

Qingming

Qīng Míng
Du Mu · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

The Qingming rain comes drizzling down without an end;

The travellers on the road are lost in grief, heart-sore.

"Where can I find a tavern?" I ask — and round the bend,

A herd-boy points far off to Apricot Village's door.

About this poem

Qingming ("Clear and Bright", early April) is the festival for sweeping ancestors' graves — a day of mourning, traditionally wet. The tavern and the apricot blossoms promise a little warmth. "Apricot Blossom Village" (杏花村) became the name of a famous liquor, and several towns still claim to be the place. The poem first appears in a Song-dynasty anthology, so its attribution to Du Mu is traditional rather than certain.
