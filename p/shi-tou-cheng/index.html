石头城

〔唐〕刘禹锡

山围故国周遭在，潮打空城寂寞回。

淮水东边旧时月，夜深还过女墙来。

英译 English translation

Stone City

Shí Tóu Chéng
Liu Yuxi · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

The hills still ring the fallen capital around;

The tide beats on the empty city, and lonely, ebbs away.

East of the Qinhuai, the moon that once shone here

Still climbs the parapets at midnight, as in the olden day.

About this poem

Stone City was the fortress of Nanjing, capital of six fallen dynasties. Only the hills, the tide and the moon — which shone on all those vanished courts — remain. Bai Juyi said that after this poem, later poets would have nothing left to say about Nanjing.

Earlier translations 旧译

“The Ruined City”W. J. B. Fletcher, 1919

By hills enclosed, surrounded by its ancient country fair,

Revealing when the tide recedes its desolation bare,

The moon that o'er the river Huai climbed up the Eastern Tower

Still comes to overpeer its wall in midnight's lonely hour.

Translated by W. J. B. Fletcher, from Gems of Chinese Verse (1919) · public domain.
