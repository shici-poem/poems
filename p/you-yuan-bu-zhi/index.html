游园不值

〔宋〕叶绍翁

应怜屐齿印苍苔，小扣柴扉久不开。

春色满园关不住，一枝红杏出墙来。

英译 English translation

Visiting a Garden, Finding No One Home

Yóu Yuán Bù Zhí
Ye Shaoweng · Song · Seven-character quatrain

Perhaps he fears my wooden clogs would mark his mossy ground;

I tap upon his wicker gate — no answer, none at all.

But a garden full of spring cannot be locked inside:

A spray of red apricot blossom reaches out over the wall.

About this poem

Visiting a friend's garden and finding no one home. The gate stays shut — but spring cannot be locked in. 春色满园关不住，一枝红杏出墙来 is quoted for anything vital and beautiful that cannot be suppressed (and, mischievously, of wandering wives).

Earlier translations 旧译

“At a Park Gate”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

'Tis closed!—lest trampling footsteps mar the glory of the green.

Time after time we knock and knock; no janitor is seen.

Yet bolts and bars can't quite shut in the spring-time's beauteous pall:

A pink-flowered almond-spray peeps out athwart the envious wall!

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
