别董大（其一）

〔唐〕高适

千里黄云白日曛，北风吹雁雪纷纷。

莫愁前路无知己，天下谁人不识君。

英译 English translation

Farewell to Dong the Elder (I)

Bié Dǒng Dà Qí Yī
Gao Shi · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

A thousand li of yellow cloud; the white sun's dim and low;

The north wind blows the wild geese on through swirling snow.

Don't fear the road ahead will hold no friend who knows your name —

Who in all the world is there who doesn't know your fame?

About this poem

Dong the Elder was Dong Tinglan, the greatest qin player of the age. Gao Shi, then poor and unknown, sees him off in a snowstorm with no gift but this encouragement — one of the most heartening farewell poems ever written.
