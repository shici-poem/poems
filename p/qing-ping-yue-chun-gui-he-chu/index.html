清平乐（春归何处）

〔宋〕黄庭坚

春归何处？寂寞无行路。

若有人知春去处，唤取归来同住。

春无踪迹谁知？除非问取黄鹂。

百啭无人能解，因风飞过蔷薇。

英译 English translation

Pure Serene Music (Where Has Spring Gone?)

Qīng Píng Yuè Chūn Guī Hé Chù
Huang Tingjian · Song · Ci lyric

Where has spring gone home?

It's lonely here — no tracks to trace.

If anyone knows where spring has gone,

Call it back to stay with us in this place.

Spring leaves no trace — who knows?

Unless you ask the oriole.

But no one understands its hundred songs —

It rides the wind across the roses, and goes.

About this poem

A playful chase after the departing spring. The only witness, the oriole, sings a hundred songs no one understands, and flies off over the roses — the flowers of early summer. Spring is gone.
