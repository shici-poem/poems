破阵子（四十年来家国）

〔五代〕李煜

四十年来家国，三千里地山河。

凤阁龙楼连霄汉，玉树琼枝作烟萝。几曾识干戈？

一旦归为臣虏，沈腰潘鬓消磨。

最是仓皇辞庙日，教坊犹奏别离歌。垂泪对宫娥。

英译 English translation

Breaking Through the Ranks (Forty Years of House and State)

Pò Zhèn Zǐ Sì Shí Nián Lái Jiā Guó
Li Yu · Five Dynasties · Ci lyric

Forty years my house and kingdom stood;

Three thousand li of mountains, rivers, lands.

Phoenix halls and dragon towers rose to touch the sky;

Jade trees and gem-like boughs were twined like misty vines.

When did I ever know of shields and spears?

One day I was a captive, bowing to the throne —

My waist grew thin, my temples grey, worn down with the years.

Worst of all, the day I left the ancestral shrine in haste,

The court musicians still were playing songs of parting —

In tears I faced my palace maids, alone.

About this poem

Li Yu looks back on the Southern Tang's forty years and its rich lands, lost when he surrendered to the Song in 975. Shen Yue grew thin and Pan Yue grey with grief — classic images of suffering. Later moralists complained that at the moment of losing his kingdom he wept to his palace maids rather than to his ancestors or people; the poem's honesty is part of its power.
