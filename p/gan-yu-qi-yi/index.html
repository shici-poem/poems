感遇（其一）

〔唐〕张九龄

兰叶春葳蕤，桂华秋皎洁。

欣欣此生意，自尔为佳节。

谁知林栖者，闻风坐相悦。

草木有本心，何求美人折？

英译 English translation

Moved by What I Encounter (I)

Gǎn Yù Qí Yī
Zhang Jiuling · Tang · Five-character old-style verse

In spring the orchid leaves grow lush and green;

In autumn cassia blossoms shine so pure and bright.

They flourish, full of life, serene,

Each in its own season, as is right.

Who'd think the hermits living in the woods

Would catch their fragrance on the wind and love them so?

But plants have their own nature — why should they

Seek to be picked by a beauty, just for show?

About this poem

The first of twelve poems written after Zhang Jiuling lost power in 737. Orchid and cassia are fragrant by their own nature, not to win admiration — like a person of virtue, who does not need to be "picked" by a ruler (the "beauty" of the old allegory). Integrity does not depend on recognition.
