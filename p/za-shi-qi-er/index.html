杂诗（其二）

〔唐〕王维

君自故乡来，应知故乡事。

来日绮窗前，寒梅著花未？

英译 English translation

Miscellaneous Poem (II)

Zá Shī Qí Èr
Wang Wei · Tang · Five-character quatrain

You've just come from our old hometown —

You must know how things there are going.

The day you left, before my latticed window,

Had the winter plum begun its blossoming?

About this poem

Of all the things he could ask about home — family, friends, the harvest — the poet asks only about one plum tree outside his window. The smallest detail carries the whole of homesickness.

Earlier translations 旧译

“A Rencontre”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

Sir, from my dear old home you come,

And all its glories you can name;

Oh tell me,—has the winter-plum

Yet blossomed o'er the window-frame?

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
