浪淘沙（其一）

〔唐〕刘禹锡

九曲黄河万里沙，浪淘风簸自天涯。

如今直上银河去，同到牵牛织女家。

英译 English translation

Waves Washing Sand (I)

Làng Táo Shā Qí Yī
Liu Yuxi · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

The Yellow River's nine great bends, ten thousand li of sand,

Washed by waves and tossed by winds, come from the edge of sky.

Now let us ride straight up the Silver River, hand in hand,

To the Herdboy's and the Weaver's home, way up high.

About this poem

An old legend held that the Yellow River joins the Milky Way (the "Silver River"), and that a man who sailed up it reached the stars. In a folk-song voice, the poet proposes to follow the river all the way to the two star-lovers.
