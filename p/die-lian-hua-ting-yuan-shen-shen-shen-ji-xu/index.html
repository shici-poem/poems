蝶恋花（庭院深深深几许）

〔宋〕欧阳修

庭院深深深几许？杨柳堆烟，帘幕无重数。

玉勒雕鞍游冶处，楼高不见章台路。

雨横风狂三月暮，门掩黄昏，无计留春住。

泪眼问花花不语，乱红飞过秋千去。

英译 English translation

Butterflies Love Flowers (Deep, Deep the Courtyard)

Dié Liàn Huā Tíng Yuàn Shēn Shēn Shēn Jǐ Xǔ
Ouyang Xiu · Song · Ci lyric

Deep, deep the courtyard — how deep can it be?

Willows heaped with mist,

And curtains past all counting, endlessly.

With jade bridle and carved saddle, he roams the pleasure lanes;

The tower's high — the road to Zhangtai is lost to me.

Rain lashing, wind raging, the third month's end;

I close the gate at dusk —

There's no way I can make the spring stay.

With tearful eyes I ask the flowers — the flowers do not speak;

The scattered red flies past the swing, and away.

About this poem

A wife shut deep in a great house while her husband rides off to the pleasure quarters (Zhangtai Lane). Li Qingzhao loved the opening line so much that she began several lyrics with it — and it is her testimony that settles the authorship, since some collections give the lyric to Feng Yansi. The last couplet — the flowers, asked, say nothing, and fly away past the swing where she once played — is one of the most admired in the ci.
