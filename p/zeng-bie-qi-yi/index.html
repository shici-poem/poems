赠别（其一）

〔唐〕杜牧

娉娉袅袅十三余，豆蔻梢头二月初。

春风十里扬州路，卷上珠帘总不如。

英译 English translation

Parting Gift (I)

Zèng Bié Qí Yī
Du Mu · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

So graceful, slender, just past thirteen years —

A cardamom bud on the bough as early spring appears.

Along ten li of Yangzhou's road in spring's warm air,

Roll up every pearl curtain — no beauty there compares.

About this poem

Written for a young singing girl as Du Mu left Yangzhou in 835. 豆蔻年华, "cardamom years" — a phrase from this poem — still means a girl of thirteen or fourteen. Behind every pearl blind on Yangzhou's famous avenue there were beauties; none matched her.
