马嵬（其二）

〔唐〕李商隐

海外徒闻更九州，他生未卜此生休。

空闻虎旅传宵柝，无复鸡人报晓筹。

此日六军同驻马，当时七夕笑牵牛。

如何四纪为天子，不及卢家有莫愁。

英译 English translation

Mawei (II)

Mǎ Wéi Qí Èr
Li Shangyin · Tang · Seven-character regulated verse

In vain they told of nine more continents across the sea —

The next life is uncertain; this one's done.

He hears the tiger guards beat out the watches, emptily;

No cockman calls the palace dawn — not one.

That day the Six Armies all reined in their horses there;

That Seventh Night, they'd laughed at the Herdboy's plight.

How could he, four decades the Son of Heaven, fare

Worse than the Lu clan, who kept Mochou home each night?

About this poem

Li Shangyin's sharp reply to the romance of The Song of Everlasting Sorrow. The tale that Consort Yang lives on in an immortals' island beyond the sea is empty talk. On the Seventh Night, when the Herdboy and the Weaver Girl meet just once a year, the imperial lovers pitied the stars — yet after nearly fifty years on the throne, Xuanzong could not protect his beloved as well as a commoner of the folk song, whose wife Mochou lived happily at home.
