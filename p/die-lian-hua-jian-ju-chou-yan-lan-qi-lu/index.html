蝶恋花（槛菊愁烟兰泣露）

〔宋〕晏殊

槛菊愁烟兰泣露，罗幕轻寒，燕子双飞去。

明月不谙离恨苦，斜光到晓穿朱户。

昨夜西风凋碧树，独上高楼，望尽天涯路。

欲寄彩笺兼尺素，山长水阔知何处？

英译 English translation

Butterflies Love Flowers (Chrysanthemums Grieve in Mist)

Dié Liàn Huā Jiàn Jú Chóu Yān Lán Qì Lù
Yan Shu · Song · Ci lyric

By the railing, chrysanthemums grieve in mist; the orchids weep with dew;

The silk curtains feel a gentle chill;

The swallows fly off, two by two.

The bright moon doesn't know the pain of being apart —

Till dawn its slanting light shines through the red door, through and through.

Last night the west wind stripped the green trees bare;

Alone I climbed the high tower,

And gazed along the road to the world's end — no one there.

I'd send a coloured note, a letter on fine silk —

But mountains are long, the waters wide: who knows where?

About this poem

昨夜西风凋碧树，独上高楼，望尽天涯路 — Wang Guowei made this the first of his "three stages" of every great achievement: climbing alone, seeing far, setting the goal. In the poem itself, it is a woman gazing down an endless road for someone who does not come.
