鹧鸪天（彩袖殷勤捧玉钟）

〔宋〕晏几道

彩袖殷勤捧玉钟，当年拚却醉颜红。

舞低杨柳楼心月，歌尽桃花扇底风。

从别后，忆相逢，几回魂梦与君同。

今宵剩把银釭照，犹恐相逢是梦中。

英译 English translation

Partridge Sky (Your Coloured Sleeves Offered the Jade Cup)

Zhè Gū Tiān Cǎi Xiù Yīn Qín Pěng Yù Zhōng
Yan Jidao · Song · Ci lyric

Your coloured sleeves so kindly offered me the cup of jade;

That year I drank without a care, my face aflame with wine.

We danced until the moon sank low above the willowed tower;

We sang until the breeze was spent from your peach-blossom fan, so fine.

Since we parted,

I've remembered how we met;

How many times my dreaming soul has been with you!

Tonight I keep on lifting up the silver lamp to see —

Afraid our meeting's just a dream, and won't come true.

About this poem

A reunion with a singing girl after years apart. The second couplet — dancing till the moon sank over the willows, singing till the breeze of the peach-blossom fan was spent — is one of the most glittering in the ci. Holding up the lamp again and again to make sure it is not a dream echoes Du Fu's reunion with his family: "we face each other as if in a dream."
