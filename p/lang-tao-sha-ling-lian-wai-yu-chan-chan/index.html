浪淘沙令（帘外雨潺潺）

〔五代〕李煜

帘外雨潺潺，春意阑珊。

罗衾不耐五更寒。

梦里不知身是客，一晌贪欢。

独自莫凭栏，无限江山。

别时容易见时难。

流水落花春去也，天上人间。

英译 English translation

Waves Washing Sand (Beyond the Blind, the Rain)

Làng Táo Shā Lìng Lián Wài Yǔ Chán Chán
Li Yu · Five Dynasties · Ci lyric

Beyond the blind, the rain is pattering;

Spring is fading fast.

My silken quilt can't keep out the cold before the dawn.

Dreaming, I forgot I was a captive guest,

And snatched a moment's joy — now past.

Alone, I must not lean upon the rail

And see the boundless land that once was mine.

Parting was easy; meeting again is hard.

The water flows, the flowers fall, the spring is gone:

Heaven above, and the world of men below.

About this poem

Written shortly before Li Yu's death. The cruellest line is the fourth: in his dream he forgot he was a prisoner and enjoyed a moment of his old life — then woke to the cold. He must not lean on the railing alone, because the view of rivers and mountains reminds him of everything he lost.
