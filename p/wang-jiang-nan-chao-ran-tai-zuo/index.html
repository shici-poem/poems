望江南·超然台作

〔宋〕苏轼

春未老，风细柳斜斜。

试上超然台上看，半壕春水一城花。烟雨暗千家。

寒食后，酒醒却咨嗟。

休对故人思故国，且将新火试新茶。诗酒趁年华。

英译 English translation

Gazing South of the River: Written on the Terrace of Transcendence

Wàng Jiāng Nán Chāo Rán Tái Zuò
Su Shi · Song · Ci lyric

Spring's not yet old;

The breeze is soft, the willows lean.

I climb the Terrace of Transcendence to behold:

Half a moat of spring water, a whole city's flowers seen,

And a thousand homes in misty rain, dim and green.

After Cold Food,

Sobered up from wine, I sigh.

Don't talk to old friends of the home we left behind;

Let's try new tea on the new fire — you and I.

Poetry and wine, while we're still young, before the years go by!

About this poem

Mizhou, 1076, on the "Terrace of Transcendence" (超然台) that Su Shi had rebuilt. After the Cold Food Festival, when fires were lit again, came the season of new tea. 诗酒趁年华, "poetry and wine, while we are young", is his cheerful remedy for homesickness.
