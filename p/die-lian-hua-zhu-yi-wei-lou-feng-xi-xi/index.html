蝶恋花（伫倚危楼风细细）

〔宋〕柳永

伫倚危楼风细细，望极春愁，黯黯生天际。

草色烟光残照里，无言谁会凭阑意。

拟把疏狂图一醉，对酒当歌，强乐还无味。

衣带渐宽终不悔，为伊消得人憔悴。

英译 English translation

Butterflies Love Flowers (Leaning Long on the High Tower)

Dié Liàn Huā Zhù Yǐ Wēi Lóu Fēng Xì Xì
Liu Yong · Song · Ci lyric

I stand and lean on the high tower in a gentle breeze;

I gaze afar — and spring's grief

Darkly rises where the earth and heaven convene.

The grass, the misty light, in the last of the sun —

Who knows why I lean here, silent, at the rail, unseen?

I thought I'd let myself go wild, and drown it all in wine;

With wine in hand I sing,

But forced joy has no taste of its own.

My belt grows looser, yet I'll never regret —

For her, I'll waste away, worn to skin and bone.

About this poem

衣带渐宽终不悔，为伊消得人憔悴 — "my belt grows ever looser, but I'll never regret it; for her I waste away" — Wang Guowei made this the second of his "three stages" of every great achievement: wholehearted, sleepless devotion.
