泊船瓜洲

〔宋〕王安石

京口瓜洲一水间，钟山只隔数重山。

春风又绿江南岸，明月何时照我还？

英译 English translation

Mooring at Guazhou

Bó Chuán Guā Zhōu
Wang Anshi · Song · Seven-character quatrain

Jingkou and Guazhou — only a river runs between;

Bell Mountain lies beyond just a few hills more.

Once again the spring wind turns the south bank green —

Bright moon, when will you light me home to my own door?

About this poem

Wang Anshi, mooring at Guazhou on the Yangtze in 1075 on his way back to office as chief minister, looks toward his retirement home at Bell Mountain (Zhongshan) in Nanjing. The verb 绿, "to green", is the most famous word choice in Chinese poetry: his draft is said to have tried "arrives", "passes", "enters", "fills" and a dozen others before he settled on "greens".
