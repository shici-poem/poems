过华清宫（其一）

〔唐〕杜牧

长安回望绣成堆，山顶千门次第开。

一骑红尘妃子笑，无人知是荔枝来。

英译 English translation

Passing Huaqing Palace (I)

Guò Huá Qīng Gōng Qí Yī
Du Mu · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

Looking back from Chang'an: hills like heaps of embroidery;

On the summit, a thousand palace gates swing open, one by one.

A horseman in red dust — the Consort smiles with glee;

No one knows it's lychees he has brought, at a run.

About this poem

Huaqing Palace on Mount Li was the winter retreat of Emperor Xuanzong and Consort Yang. She loved fresh lychees, so relays of horsemen galloped them a thousand miles from the far south, killing horses on the way, so they would arrive fresh. A whole empire's waste, in one smile.
