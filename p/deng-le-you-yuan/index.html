登乐游原

〔唐〕李商隐

向晚意不适，驱车登古原。

夕阳无限好，只是近黄昏。

英译 English translation

Climbing Leyou Plain

Dēng Lè Yóu Yuán
Li Shangyin · Tang · Five-character quatrain

Toward evening, my heart was ill at ease;

I drove my carriage up the ancient height.

The setting sun is boundlessly lovely —

It's just that it's so close to night.

About this poem

Leyou Plain was the highest spot in Chang'an, with a view over the whole city. 夕阳无限好，只是近黄昏 — beauty at its height is already ending — is quoted of old age, of the late Tang dynasty itself, and of every last golden moment.

Earlier translations 旧译

“The Night Comes”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

'Tis evening, and in restless vein

At the old mount I slacken rein:

The glorious day

Fades fast away

And naught but twilight glooms remain!

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
