渔家傲（天接云涛连晓雾）

〔宋〕李清照

天接云涛连晓雾，星河欲转千帆舞。

仿佛梦魂归帝所。闻天语，殷勤问我归何处。

我报路长嗟日暮，学诗谩有惊人句。

九万里风鹏正举。风休住，蓬舟吹取三山去！

英译 English translation

The Fisherman's Pride (The Sky Meets the Waves of Cloud)

Yú Jiā Ào Tiān Jiē Yún Tāo Lián Xiǎo Wù
Li Qingzhao · Song · Ci lyric

The sky meets the waves of cloud, and joins the morning mist;

The Milky Way begins to turn; a thousand sails dance high.

As if my dreaming soul had gone back to the Emperor's hall,

I hear a voice from heaven

Asking, kindly, where I'm going, and why.

I answer: "The road is long; alas, the sun is low;

I learned to write, but my startling lines are all in vain."

On ninety thousand li of wind, the great Peng bird soars high —

Wind, don't stop!

Blow my little boat to the Isles of the Immortals, far from pain!

About this poem

Li Qingzhao's one great "heroic" lyric, written in her refugee years: a dream voyage among the stars, where the Emperor of Heaven asks where she is bound. The Peng is the colossal bird of Zhuangzi that rides the wind ninety thousand li; the Three Mountains are the islands of the immortals. Critics said it could pass for Su Shi.
