无题（来是空言去绝踪）

〔唐〕李商隐

来是空言去绝踪，月斜楼上五更钟。

梦为远别啼难唤，书被催成墨未浓。

蜡照半笼金翡翠，麝熏微度绣芙蓉。

刘郎已恨蓬山远，更隔蓬山一万重。

英译 English translation

Untitled (Your Coming Was an Empty Word)

Wú Tí Lái Shì Kōng Yán Qù Jué Zōng
Li Shangyin · Tang · Seven-character regulated verse

Your coming was an empty word; you've gone without a trace;

The moon slants on the tower; the fifth-watch bell is tolled.

In dreams we parted far; I wept, and could not call or hold;

I rushed this letter — the ink's too pale, not thick nor bold.

The candlelight half-veils the gold kingfisher quilt;

The musk scent faintly drifts through the embroidered lotus' fold.

Young Liu already grieved that Penglai Mountain was so far —

And you're beyond Penglai by ten thousand mountains' fold.

About this poem

In a Han legend, Liu Chen met two fairies in the Tiantai Mountains, stayed half a year, and returned to find seven generations had passed — he could never find them again. The lover is now even further than that fairyland. The candle half-lighting the kingfisher-embroidered quilt and the drifting musk are the traces of a night together.
