山中问答

〔唐〕李白

问余何意栖碧山，笑而不答心自闲。

桃花流水窅然去，别有天地非人间。

英译 English translation

Question and Answer in the Mountains

Shān Zhōng Wèn Dá
Li Bai · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

You ask me why I dwell among the green hills here;

I smile and don't reply — my heart is calm and free.

Peach blossoms on the water drift away, and disappear:

There is another world, not of men, for me.

About this poem

Peach blossoms floating on a stream recall Tao Yuanming's Peach Blossom Spring, a hidden paradise found by following petals upstream. The best answer to "why live in the mountains?" is a smile.

Earlier translations 旧译

“The Poet”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

You ask what my soul does away in the sky,

I inwardly smile but I cannot reply;

Like the peach-blossom carried away by the stream,

I soar to a world of which you cannot dream.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
