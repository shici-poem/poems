无题（相见时难别亦难）

〔唐〕李商隐

相见时难别亦难，东风无力百花残。

春蚕到死丝方尽，蜡炬成灰泪始干。

晓镜但愁云鬓改，夜吟应觉月光寒。

蓬山此去无多路，青鸟殷勤为探看。

英译 English translation

Untitled (Hard It Is to Meet)

Wú Tí Xiāng Jiàn Shí Nán Bié Yì Nán
Li Shangyin · Tang · Seven-character regulated verse

It's hard for us to meet, and just as hard to part;

The east wind has no strength; a hundred flowers fall.

The spring silkworm spins its silk until it dies;

The candle weeps till it is ash, its tears dried after all.

At dawn, before her mirror, she grieves her hair turning grey;

Chanting at night, she must feel the moonlight's cold.

From here to Penglai Mountain is not so far a way —

Bluebird, go faithfully, and see her for me, as of old.

About this poem

The most famous of Li Shangyin's "Untitled" poems. The silkworm and the candle are the supreme images of devotion until death — and 丝 "silk" puns on 思 "longing". The bluebird was the messenger of the Queen Mother of the West, and Penglai is the island of immortals: the beloved is as unreachable as a goddess. Teachers today quote the couplet to honour selfless dedication.
