杂诗（其一）

〔魏晋〕陶渊明

人生无根蒂，飘如陌上尘。

分散逐风转，此已非常身。

落地为兄弟，何必骨肉亲！

得欢当作乐，斗酒聚比邻。

盛年不重来，一日难再晨。

及时当勉励，岁月不待人。

英译 English translation

Miscellaneous Poem (I)

Zá Shī Qí Yī
Tao Yuanming · Wei & Jin · Five-character old-style verse

Our life has neither root nor stem;

We drift like dust upon the way;

Scattered, whirled by every wind,

We're not the selves we were yesterday.

Once born, all men are brothers —

Why care only for one's own kin?

When joy comes, let us make merry,

And call the neighbours for a jug of wine within.

Our prime will never come again;

A day has only one dawn, then it's done.

Seize the time, and strive while you can —

The years will wait for no one.

About this poem

Life is dust on the road, so all people are brothers, and joy should be shared now. The last four lines — 盛年不重来，一日难再晨。及时当勉励，岁月不待人 — are quoted to students to urge diligence, though Tao probably meant something closer to "enjoy life while you can".
