逢入京使

〔唐〕岑参

故园东望路漫漫，双袖龙钟泪不干。

马上相逢无纸笔，凭君传语报平安。

英译 English translation

Meeting an Envoy Bound for the Capital

Féng Rù Jīng Shǐ
Cen Shen · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

I gaze east toward my home — the road goes on and on;

Both my sleeves are soaked with tears that never dry.

We meet on horseback — no brush, no paper, none;

Please tell my family I'm safe and well, as you pass by.

About this poem

On his way to the Central Asian frontier in 749, Cen Shen meets an envoy heading back to Chang'an. No time to write — only a spoken message: "I'm safe." The simplest words carry the deepest feeling.
