客中作

〔唐〕李白

兰陵美酒郁金香，玉碗盛来琥珀光。

但使主人能醉客，不知何处是他乡。

英译 English translation

Written as a Traveller

Kè Zhōng Zuò
Li Bai · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

The fine wine of Lanling breathes of turmeric's scent;

Poured in a bowl of jade, it gleams with amber light.

If only my host can get his guest well drunk tonight,

I'll never know which land is foreign, or where I went.

About this poem

Lanling (in Shandong) was famous for its wine, scented with 郁金 (turmeric or curcuma). Most travel poems are homesick; this one, from Li Bai's carefree younger days, cheerfully proposes that good enough wine makes anywhere home.

Earlier translations 旧译

“In Exile”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

I drink deep draughts of Lan-ling wine fragrant with borage made,

The liquid amber mantling up in cups of costly jade.

My host insists on making me as drunk as any sot,

Until I'm quite oblivious of the exile's wretched lot.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
