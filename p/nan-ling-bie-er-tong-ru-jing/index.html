南陵别儿童入京

〔唐〕李白

白酒新熟山中归，黄鸡啄黍秋正肥。

呼童烹鸡酌白酒，儿女嬉笑牵人衣。

高歌取醉欲自慰，起舞落日争光辉。

游说万乘苦不早，著鞭跨马涉远道。

会稽愚妇轻买臣，余亦辞家西入秦。

仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人。

英译 English translation

Leaving My Children at Nanling for the Capital

Nán Líng Bié Ér Tóng Rù Jīng
Li Bai · Tang · Seven-character old-style verse

The white wine has just ripened as I come back to the hills;

The yellow hens peck millet, in the autumn fat and fine.

I call the boy to cook a chicken and to pour the wine;

My children laugh and tug my clothes with giggles and with trills.

I sing aloud and drink my fill to soothe my soul;

I rise to dance, and vie in brilliance with the setting sun.

Too late, I've come to counsel the Lord of Ten Thousand Chariots — the goal!

I whip my horse and ride the long road till it's done.

The foolish wife of Kuaiji scorned Zhu Maichen once, they say;

I, too, now leave my home and go west, to Qin, away.

I throw back my head and laugh aloud, and out the gate I stride —

Are men like me to live and die in brambles, cast aside?

About this poem

In 742, aged 41, Li Bai was summoned to court by Emperor Xuanzong. Zhu Maichen was a Han woodcutter whose wife left him for his poverty — and who later rose to high office. The last couplet, a shout of vindication, is one of the most quoted lines Li Bai wrote. (The court career lasted less than two years.)
