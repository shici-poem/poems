清平乐·村居

〔宋〕辛弃疾

茅檐低小，溪上青青草。

醉里吴音相媚好，白发谁家翁媪？

大儿锄豆溪东，中儿正织鸡笼。

最喜小儿亡赖，溪头卧剥莲蓬。

英译 English translation

Pure Serene Music: Village Life

Qīng Píng Yuè Cūn Jū
Xin Qiji · Song · Ci lyric

The thatched eaves are low and small;

Along the creek the grass grows green.

Tipsy, in soft Wu dialect, so sweet to hear —

Whose white-haired couple is this I've seen?

The eldest boy is hoeing beans east of the stream;

The middle one is weaving a coop for the hens.

But best of all, the youngest, the little rascal,

Sprawled by the creek, peeling lotus pods.

About this poem

A happy farm family seen by the retired general: an old couple chatting tipsily in their soft local dialect, and three sons — two hard at work, and the youngest, a lazy scamp, lying by the creek snacking on lotus seeds. Every Chinese child learns it.
