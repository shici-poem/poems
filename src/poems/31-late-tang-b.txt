== 不第后赋菊 | On Chrysanthemums, After Failing the Examination
by: 黄巢
form: 七绝
tags: 花 秋 壮志 战争
scene: autumn chrysanthemum wall flag wind
待到秋来九月八，我花开后百花杀。
冲天香阵透长安，满城尽带黄金甲。
--
Wait till the autumn comes — the ninth month, the eighth day:
When my flowers open, all the hundred flowers die.
Their fragrance, storming heaven, will sweep through Chang'an's every way,
And the whole city will wear gold armour, gleaming high.
--
Written by Huang Chao after failing the imperial examination. The chrysanthemum, which blooms at the Double Ninth, becomes an army: its golden petals are armour, its fragrance an assault on the capital. In 880 Huang Chao's rebels really did take Chang'an — the poem reads as a prophecy.

== 咸阳城东楼 | The East Tower of Xianyang
by: 许浑
form: 七律
tags: 怀古 风 雨 秋
scene: dusk autumn wind tower wall river reeds willow clouds
一上高城万里愁，蒹葭杨柳似汀洲。
溪云初起日沉阁，山雨欲来风满楼。
鸟下绿芜秦苑夕，蝉鸣黄叶汉宫秋。
行人莫问当年事，故国东来渭水流。
--
I climb the city wall, and grief spreads ten thousand li;
The reeds and willows look like islets on a southern shore.
Clouds rise from the stream; the sun sinks behind the hall's high eaves;
The mountain rain is coming — wind fills the tower's every door.
Birds land in the green weeds of the Qin park as evening falls;
Cicadas cry in yellow leaves where Han palaces stood before.
Traveller, don't ask about the things of long ago —
Past the old capital, from the east, the Wei flows as of yore.
--
Xianyang, capital of the Qin, lies across the Wei River from Chang'an. 山雨欲来风满楼, "the mountain rain is coming; the wind fills the tower", became the proverb for the tense moment before a storm breaks — political or otherwise. The ruined Qin and Han palaces foreshadow the fall of the Tang itself.

== 贫女 | The Poor Girl
by: 秦韬玉
form: 七律
tags: 女性 失意 民生
scene: day cottage window person candle
蓬门未识绮罗香，拟托良媒益自伤。
谁爱风流高格调，共怜时世俭梳妆。
敢将十指夸针巧，不把双眉斗画长。
苦恨年年压金线，为他人作嫁衣裳。
--
Born behind a wicker gate, I've never known the scent of silk;
I'd seek a matchmaker — but that would only hurt me more.
Who loves a noble manner and a lofty, gentle style?
They all prefer the cheap and showy fashions everyone adores.
I dare to boast my fingers have a needle's matchless art;
I won't compete in painting eyebrows long, to steal a heart.
Year after year I bitterly press gold thread — and it's to make
The wedding dresses other girls will wear, for others' sake.
--
A poor, skilled, virtuous girl whom no one marries, while she sews bridal gowns for others — an allegory of the talented scholar without connections, whose work serves other men's advancement. 为他人作嫁衣裳, "making wedding clothes for others", is now a common idiom.

== 山亭夏日 | Summer Day at the Mountain Pavilion
by: 高骈
form: 七绝
tags: 夏 闲适 花 风
scene: summer pavilion lake flowers wind pine
绿树阴浓夏日长，楼台倒影入池塘。
水晶帘动微风起，满架蔷薇一院香。
--
Green trees cast heavy shade; the summer days are long;
The tower's reflection falls upon the pond.
The crystal curtain stirs: a breeze begins to blow;
A trellis full of roses scents the courtyard all around.
--
A cool summer afternoon at a mountain pavilion: shade, reflection, a stirring curtain — and then the scent of roses fills the courtyard. The breeze is never seen, only inferred from the curtain and the perfume.

== 己亥岁（其一） | The Year Jihai (I)
by: 曹松
form: 七绝
tags: 战争 民生 哲理
scene: dusk river hills fort grass
泽国江山入战图，生民何计乐樵苏。
凭君莫话封侯事，一将功成万骨枯。
--
The rivers and the hills down south are drawn on maps of war;
How can the people live in peace, cutting firewood and grass?
I beg you, don't speak to me of lordships won in war —
One general's fame is built upon ten thousand bones, alas.
--
Written in 879, as the Tang fought Huang Chao's rebels across the south. 一将功成万骨枯, "one general's success, ten thousand withered bones", became the most quoted line against war in Chinese.
