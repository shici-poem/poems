== 春晓 | Spring Morning
by: 孟浩然
form: 五绝
tags: 春 花 鸟 雨 风
scene: dawn spring rain petals birds cottage window
春眠不觉晓，处处闻啼鸟。
夜来风雨声，花落知多少。
--
In spring I sleep and miss the break of day;
Everywhere I hear the birds sing on.
Last night, the sound of wind and rain —
How many blossoms fell before the dawn?
--
After *Quiet Night Thoughts*, perhaps the most famous poem in Chinese. Half awake on a spring morning, the poet hears birdsong and remembers the night's storm: a moment of pure contentment shadowed by the thought of fallen petals — beauty and its passing in twenty characters.
--
写作年代已不可考，诗意闲适，一般认为是孟浩然早年在家乡襄阳隐居时所作。春日酣眠，不觉天已破晓，处处鸟声入耳；忽然想起夜里的风雨声，不知又吹落了多少花。诗从听觉落笔，不写一眼所见，满心惬意里透出一丝惜春之情。语言如脱口而出，自然浑成，二十个字写尽美好与凋零。

== 过故人庄 | Visiting an Old Friend's Farm
by: 孟浩然
form: 五律
tags: 田园 友情 饮酒 节日 秋
scene: autumn hills cottage fields chrysanthemum two jar
故人具鸡黍，邀我至田家。
绿树村边合，青山郭外斜。
开轩面场圃，把酒话桑麻。
待到重阳日，还来就菊花。
--
My old friend made a meal of chicken and millet,
And invited me to his farm to dine.
Green trees encircle the village;
Blue hills slope beyond the town line.
We open the window on the threshing yard and garden,
And over wine we talk of mulberry and hemp.
When the Double Ninth comes round again,
I'll come back for the chrysanthemums — I won't be exempt!
--
The great pastoral poem of the Tang. Everything is plain — chicken and millet, talk of crops — and that is the joy. The promise to return on the Double Ninth, when people drank chrysanthemum wine, is the warmest possible thank-you.
--
孟浩然一生大半隐居故乡襄阳，这首诗写他应邀到农家老友家做客。故人备好鸡黍相邀，绿树环抱村庄，青山斜倚城外；推开窗子面对场院菜圃，端起酒杯闲话桑麻。全诗没有一个华丽的字，处处是田家的质朴与友情的真淳，被视为唐代田园诗的代表作。末句约定重阳节再来赏菊，一个“就”字，写出主客之间的亲近与随意。（“鸡黍”是农家待客的饭菜，《论语》中隐者款待子路，便是“杀鸡为黍而食之”；“场圃”是打谷场和菜园。）

== 宿建德江 | Mooring on the Jiande River
by: 孟浩然
form: 五绝
tags: 羁旅 月 江河 思乡 夜
scene: dusk moon river boat mist hills
移舟泊烟渚，日暮客愁新。
野旷天低树，江清月近人。
--
I steer my boat to moor by a misty isle;
As the sun sets, a traveller's grief comes new.
The plain is vast — the sky stoops below the trees;
The river's clear — the moon comes close to me, too.
--
Mooring at dusk on the Xin'an River in Zhejiang. The last two lines are among the most admired in Chinese: on the open plain the sky seems lower than the trees; in the clear river, the moon's reflection floats right beside the boat — the only companion the lonely traveller has.
--
建德江是新安江流经建德（今属浙江）的一段。孟浩然四十岁赴长安应试落第，此后南下漫游吴越，这首诗一般认为就作于那段旅途中。日暮时分，小船停靠在烟雾迷蒙的沙洲边，客愁又添了新的一层。后两句写景极妙：原野空旷，远处的天空看上去比近树还低；江水清澈，水中的月影仿佛就近在身旁。天地之大更显人的孤独，而唯一亲近旅人的，只有那一轮江月。“愁”字只出现一次，却笼罩了全诗。

== 望洞庭湖赠张丞相 | Gazing at Dongting Lake: To Chancellor Zhang
by: 孟浩然
form: 五律
tags: 山水 壮志 失意 江河
scene: autumn lake wall fisher boat mist
八月湖水平，涵虚混太清。
气蒸云梦泽，波撼岳阳城。
欲济无舟楫，端居耻圣明。
坐观垂钓者，徒有羡鱼情。
--
In the eighth month, the lake brims level with its shore;
It holds the void, and merges with the sky.
Its vapours steam over the Yunmeng Marsh;
Its waves shake Yueyang's walls on high.
I'd cross, but have no boat or oar;
To sit idle in this age of sages would be shame.
I sit and watch the men who fish,
And only envy the fish they claim.
--
A poem asking the chief minister Zhang (probably Zhang Jiuling) for an official post — but it is the first half that made it famous: the colossal lake in autumn flood, steaming over the marshes and shaking the city walls. The second half pleads, gracefully: "I'd cross over (into office) but have no boat; watching others fish, I can only envy their catch."
--
这是一首献给宰相、希望得到引荐的干谒诗，“张丞相”通常认为是张九龄，一说是张说。前四句写八月洞庭秋水盛涨，湖天相接，浑然一体；“气蒸云梦泽，波撼岳阳城”，水汽蒸腾笼罩云梦大泽，波涛摇撼岳阳城郭，是历代写洞庭最雄浑的名句之一。后四句才转入正题：想渡湖却没有舟楫，闲居又愧对圣明之世，只能坐看别人垂钓，空怀羡鱼之情。以渡水无舟喻无人引荐，以羡鱼喻渴望出仕，求人而不失身份，委婉得体。（末两句化用古语“临渊羡鱼，不如退而结网”。）

== 岁暮归南山 | Returning to the Southern Hills at Year's End
by: 孟浩然
form: 五律
tags: 失意 冬 隐逸 月 夜
scene: night winter moon pine window cottage
北阙休上书，南山归敝庐。
不才明主弃，多病故人疏。
白发催年老，青阳逼岁除。
永怀愁不寐，松月夜窗虚。
--
No more petitions to the northern palace gate;
I'm going home to my poor hut in the southern hills.
Lacking talent, I'm cast off by our wise ruler;
Often sick, I'm left by old friends, as old age fills.
White hairs hurry my years toward age;
The green spring sun drives out the year at last.
Brooding long, I lie awake in sorrow —
Pine and moon, through the empty window, cast.
--
Legend says that Meng Haoran was hiding in Wang Wei's office when the emperor walked in, and was made to recite a poem; he chose this one — and when the emperor heard "lacking talent, I'm cast off by our wise ruler", he said, "You never sought office; I never cast you off. Why slander me?" and sent him home. Whether true or not, it is a perfect story of a poet undone by a line.
--
孟浩然年近四十才到长安求取功名，终究失意而归，此诗写的就是岁末还乡时的心情。“北阙”是宫殿北面的门楼，古时臣子在此上书奏事；不再上书了，回南山的破旧茅庐去吧。“不才明主弃，多病故人疏”，表面自谦，底下满是牢骚。《新唐书》记载：王维私下邀孟浩然进入官署，恰逢玄宗驾到，孟浩然慌忙躲到床下；玄宗召他出来诵诗，听到这一句，便说你自己不求仕进，朕何曾弃你，为何诬我？于是放他回乡。故事真假难考，却让这一句流传千古。末句长夜不眠，只见松间月色照着空窗，愁怀难遣。
