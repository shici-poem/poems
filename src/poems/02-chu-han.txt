== 离骚（节选一） | Encountering Sorrow (excerpt I)
by: 屈原
form: 楚辞
tags: 失意 爱国 民生 壮志
scene: dusk river person reeds
长太息以掩涕兮，哀民生之多艰。
余虽好修姱以鞿羁兮，謇朝谇而夕替。
既替余以蕙纕兮，又申之以揽茝。
亦余心之所善兮，虽九死其犹未悔。
--
I heave a long sigh and wipe away my tears,
Grieving that the people's lives are so hard.
Though I loved what's fine, and held myself in check,
At dawn I spoke my mind — by dusk I was barred.
They cast me out for wearing melilot,
And then for gathering angelica, too.
But this is what my heart holds dearest —
Though I die nine deaths, I'll not regret what's true.
--
From *Encountering Sorrow* (离骚), Qu Yuan's 373-line masterpiece, the longest lyric poem of ancient China, written after he was slandered and banished by the King of Chu. Fragrant plants — melilot, angelica — stand for his virtue. 虽九死其犹未悔, "though I die nine times, I will not regret it", is the creed of every principled dissenter in Chinese history.
--
屈原是战国时楚国的贵族，曾受楚怀王信任，后遭谗言而被疏远，晚年又被放逐江南。《离骚》是他的代表作，通常被称为我国古代最长的抒情诗，司马迁解释说：“离骚者，犹离忧也。”这几句里，诗人掩面长叹，哀叹人生多艰；自己爱好修洁，反受牵累，早上进谏，晚上就被罢黜；因佩戴蕙草、采集白芷而获罪，也不改初心。“亦余心之所善兮，虽九死其犹未悔”，成了历代志士坚守信念的誓言。（“民生”一说指人生，一说指百姓；“谇”是进谏，“替”是废弃。）

== 垓下歌 | Song of Gaixia
by: 项羽
form: 杂言
tags: 怀古 战争 爱情 壮志
scene: night moon fort flag horse two
力拔山兮气盖世。时不利兮骓不逝。
骓不逝兮可奈何！虞兮虞兮奈若何！
--
My strength could tear up mountains; my spirit overtopped the world;
But the times are against me, and my dappled steed won't go.
My steed won't go — what can be done?
Yu, my Yu — what will become of you now?
--
In 202 BC, surrounded at Gaixia by Liu Bang's armies and hearing the songs of his own homeland, Chu, sung in the enemy camp (四面楚歌, "Chu songs on all sides", now the idiom for being besieged), Xiang Yu drank with his beloved consort Yu and sang this song. She is said to have killed herself; he broke out, fought his last battle and died at the Wu River. The scene is the subject of the opera *Farewell My Concubine*.
--
公元前202年，项羽被刘邦大军围困在垓下（在今安徽境内），兵少粮尽，夜里听见汉军四面都唱起楚歌，大惊：汉军难道已经尽得楚地了吗？他起身在帐中饮酒，面对常随左右的美人虞和心爱的骏马骓，慷慨悲歌，虞姬随声应和，项羽泪下数行，左右无不哭泣，事见《史记·项羽本纪》。首句自负力能拔山、气可盖世，次句却是时运不济，连骓马也不肯前行；末了一声“虞兮虞兮奈若何”，英雄末路的无奈尽在其中。“四面楚歌”由此成为成语，京剧《霸王别姬》演的正是这一幕。

== 大风歌 | Song of the Great Wind
by: 刘邦
form: 杂言
tags: 壮志 思乡 怀古 风
scene: day wind clouds palace flag
大风起兮云飞扬。
威加海内兮归故乡。
安得猛士兮守四方！
--
A great wind rises; the clouds fly far and wide.
My power fills the world; I come back home with pride.
Where shall I find brave men to guard the land on every side?
--
In 195 BC, returning as emperor to his home town of Pei after crushing a rebellion, Liu Bang feasted his old neighbours, struck a zither, and sang this song while 120 local children joined in; then he danced, and wept. Triumph and anxiety in three lines: he has won the empire — but who will hold it? He died a few months later.
--
汉高祖十二年（前195），刘邦亲征平定淮南王英布的叛乱，回师途中经过故乡沛县，在沛宫置酒，召来故人父老一同畅饮，又挑选沛中少年一百二十人，教他们唱歌。酒酣之际，刘邦击筑而歌，唱的就是这首《大风歌》；随后起身起舞，慷慨伤怀，泪下数行，事见《史记·高祖本纪》。首句以大风起、云飞扬写天下风云激荡，次句写威加海内、荣归故里的豪情，末句却转为隐忧：怎样才能得到猛士守卫四方？几个月后，刘邦就去世了。

== 北方有佳人 | In the North There Is a Beauty
by: 李延年
form: 乐府
tags: 女性 爱情 音乐
scene: day palace person flowers
北方有佳人，绝世而独立。
一顾倾人城，再顾倾人国。
宁不知倾城与倾国，佳人难再得。
--
In the north there lives a lovely maid,
Peerless in all the world, she stands alone.
One glance from her, and a city falls;
A second glance, and a kingdom's overthrown.
Who would not risk a city or a kingdom?
A beauty like her is not twice known.
--
Sung by Li Yannian, a musician at the court of Emperor Wu of Han around 100 BC — about his own sister. The emperor asked whether such a woman existed; she was brought to him and became his beloved Lady Li. 倾城倾国, "toppling cities and states", became the stock phrase for supreme beauty.
--
事见《汉书·外戚传》。李延年是汉武帝宫中的乐师，精通音律，善作新声。一日他在武帝面前起舞，唱了这首歌，武帝听罢感叹：世上难道真有这样的人？平阳公主趁机说起李延年的妹妹，武帝召来一见，果然妙丽善舞，从此宠爱有加，这便是李夫人。佳人回眸一顾，城为之倾，再顾，国为之倾；明知会倾城倾国，仍叹这样的佳人难再得。“倾城倾国”从此成了形容绝色的成语。

== 长歌行 | A Long Song
by: 汉乐府
form: 乐府
tags: 时光 哲理 人生 春 秋
scene: dawn spring sun flowers river cottage
青青园中葵，朝露待日晞。
阳春布德泽，万物生光辉。
常恐秋节至，焜黄华叶衰。
百川东到海，何时复西归？
少壮不努力，老大徒伤悲！
--
In the garden, the mallows grow green;
The morning dew waits for the sun to dry.
Bright spring spreads its blessings wide,
And all things shine beneath the sky.
But I fear the autumn season's coming,
When flowers and leaves turn yellow and die.
A hundred rivers flow east to the sea —
When will they ever flow back to the west?
If you don't strive while young and strong,
You'll grieve in vain when old, with no rest.
--
An anonymous Han ballad. 少壮不努力，老大徒伤悲 — "if you don't work hard when young, you'll grieve in vain when old" — is one of the most quoted proverbs in Chinese, painted on school walls everywhere.
--
汉代乐府民歌，宋人郭茂倩《乐府诗集》将它归入“相和歌辞·平调曲”。园中的葵是汉代常吃的葵菜（冬葵），并非后来从美洲传入的向日葵。诗从朝露、春光写到秋来叶黄，再写百川东流、一去不返，层层比喻时光易逝。最后两句水到渠成，“少壮不努力，老大徒伤悲”，两千年来一直是劝人珍惜青春、及时努力的格言。

== 江南 | South of the River
by: 汉乐府
form: 乐府
tags: 夏 花 童趣 江河
scene: summer lotus lake boat lady
江南可采莲，莲叶何田田。
鱼戏莲叶间。
鱼戏莲叶东，鱼戏莲叶西，
鱼戏莲叶南，鱼戏莲叶北。
--
South of the River, we can pick the lotus;
How lush the lotus leaves, how round!
Fish play among the lotus leaves:
Fish play east of the lotus leaves,
Fish play west of the lotus leaves,
Fish play south of the lotus leaves,
Fish play north of the lotus leaves — all around!
--
An anonymous Han lotus-picking song. The last four lines, naming the four directions, may have been sung by a chorus answering a lead singer, the pickers' boats darting among the leaves like the fish. Pure joy, with no "meaning" at all.
--
汉代采莲歌，收入《乐府诗集》“相和歌辞”。相和歌一说是一人领唱、众人相和，有学者认为末四句便是众人的和声。“田田”写莲叶饱满、层层铺满水面；后面东、西、南、北四句几乎一字不换，鱼儿穿梭往来，采莲人的小船和笑语也仿佛就在其间。唐人吴兢说此诗意在“美芳晨丽景，嬉游得时”；也有人认为鱼戏莲叶暗喻男女相悦。无论哪种读法，它都是一首单纯明快的劳动之歌。

== 上邪 | O Heaven Above!
by: 汉乐府
form: 乐府
tags: 爱情 女性 哲理
scene: day mountains river snow clouds
上邪！我欲与君相知，长命无绝衰。
山无陵，江水为竭，冬雷震震，夏雨雪，天地合，乃敢与君绝！
--
O Heaven above!
I want to love you and be loved,
Forever, never fading, never done.
Only when the mountains are worn flat,
And the rivers all run dry,
When thunder rolls in winter,
And snow falls in summer,
When heaven and earth are joined in one —
Only then will I dare to part from you!
--
An anonymous Han love oath in a woman's voice. The impossibilities she lists — mountains levelled, rivers dried, winter thunder, summer snow, heaven and earth merged — make it the most passionate vow of love in Chinese.
--
汉乐府《铙歌十八曲》中的一首，是一位女子对天立下的爱情誓言，“上邪”就是“天啊”。她先发愿与心上人相知相爱，永不衰绝；接着一口气列出五件绝不可能发生的事：高山夷为平地，江水枯竭，冬天打雷，夏天下雪，天地合而为一——到那时才敢与你分离。以不可能之事反衬爱情的坚贞，语气斩钉截铁，感情炽烈奔放。唐代敦煌曲子词《菩萨蛮·枕前发尽千般愿》也用一连串不可能之事起誓，构思与此异曲同工。

== 十五从军征 | At Fifteen I Went to War
by: 汉乐府
form: 乐府
tags: 战争 思乡 民生 悼亡
scene: dusk cottage grass person pine
十五从军征，八十始得归。
道逢乡里人：家中有阿谁？
遥看是君家，松柏冢累累。
兔从狗窦入，雉从梁上飞。
中庭生旅谷，井上生旅葵。
舂谷持作饭，采葵持作羹。
羹饭一时熟，不知贻阿谁！
出门东向看，泪落沾我衣。
--
At fifteen I went off to war;
At eighty I came home again.
On the road I met a man from my village:
"Who is left at home?" I asked him then.
"Over there's your house, far off —
Among the pines and cypresses, graves in a row."
Hares come in through the dog-hole;
Pheasants fly from the beams, to and fro.
In the courtyard, wild grain grows;
By the well, wild mallows spread.
I husked the grain to cook some rice;
I picked the mallows for soup instead.
Soup and rice were soon both done —
But who was there to give them to?
I went out of the gate and looked to the east,
And my tears fell, and soaked my clothes right through.
--
An anonymous Han ballad. A man conscripted at fifteen comes home at eighty to find his whole family dead and his house a ruin overrun by animals and wild plants. He cooks a meal — and there is no one to share it with. One of the most devastating anti-war poems ever written, in the plainest words.
--
见于宋人郭茂倩所编《乐府诗集》的“梁鼓角横吹曲”，一般归入汉乐府民歌。十五岁从军，八十岁才得回乡，一生都耗在了征戍之中。路遇同乡，开口便问“家中有阿谁”，换来的回答却是松柏掩映下的累累坟冢。野兔从狗洞钻进，野鸡在梁上飞，庭中井边长满了野谷、野葵（“旅”指不经播种而自生）。老人舂谷采葵，做好了饭羹，却不知端给谁吃，只能出门东望，泪湿衣襟。全诗不发一句议论，平平叙来，兵役之苦、人生之悲已尽在其中。

== 行行重行行 | On and On You Go
by: 古诗十九首
form: 五古
tags: 相思 离别 闺怨 女性
scene: dusk clouds sun person horse birds
行行重行行，与君生别离。
相去万余里，各在天一涯。
道路阻且长，会面安可知？
胡马依北风，越鸟巢南枝。
相去日已远，衣带日已缓。
浮云蔽白日，游子不顾反。
思君令人老，岁月忽已晚。
弃捐勿复道，努力加餐饭。
--
On and on you go, and on and on,
And we're parted, though we both still live.
Ten thousand li and more between us lie,
Each of us at one far edge of the sky.
The road is hard and long, my friend —
Who knows if we shall meet again?
The northern horse still leans into the north wind;
The southern bird still nests on the southern bough.
Each day the distance grows, and now
Each day my belt hangs looser.
The floating clouds have covered the white sun;
The wanderer has no thought of coming home.
Thinking of you has made me old;
The year grows late — and I'm alone.
Enough — I'll say no more of it.
Take care, and try to eat a little more.
--
The first of the *Nineteen Old Poems* of the late Han. A wife writes to her absent husband — or a woman to a lover who has wandered off and may not return ("floating clouds hiding the sun" hints at another woman). Even horses and birds long for home. The famous ending turns from reproach to tenderness: forget what I said — just look after yourself and eat well.
--
《古诗十九首》最早见于萧统《文选》，一般认为出自东汉后期无名文人之手，刘勰誉之为“五言之冠冕”。这是第一首，写思妇怀念远行之人。开篇五字叠用四个“行”，越走越远之感扑面而来。“胡马依北风，越鸟巢南枝”，鸟兽尚且眷恋故土，远人怎会不念家？“浮云蔽白日”，或说暗指游子另有所恋，或说是他为谗言所惑。“衣带日已缓”写人渐消瘦，比直说憔悴更动人。末了满腹怨思一笔放下，只道一句“努力加餐饭”，情深而语淡。（这句一说是劝对方保重，一说是自我宽解。）

== 迢迢牵牛星 | Far, Far Away, the Herdboy Star
by: 古诗十九首
form: 五古
tags: 爱情 相思 天空 夜 女性
scene: night stars person window
迢迢牵牛星，皎皎河汉女。
纤纤擢素手，札札弄机杼。
终日不成章，泣涕零如雨。
河汉清且浅，相去复几许？
盈盈一水间，脉脉不得语。
--
Far, far away, the Herdboy Star;
Bright, bright, the Weaver Girl beside the Silver River.
Slender, slender, she raises her pale hands;
Click, clack, her shuttle flies, her loom-threads quiver.
All day long, no pattern is complete;
Her tears fall down like rain.
The Silver River's clear and shallow —
How far apart are they, the twain?
Across a single brimming stream,
They gaze in silence, and cannot speak again.
--
The legend of the Herdboy (Altair) and the Weaver Girl (Vega), lovers separated by the Milky Way, told in ten lines full of doubled words (迢迢, 皎皎, 纤纤, 札札, 盈盈, 脉脉) that sound like weaving itself. The river between them is "clear and shallow" — and yet uncrossable. The source of every Seventh Night poem that followed.
--
《古诗十九首》的第十首，出自东汉无名文人之手。《诗经·大东》里，织女、牵牛还只是两颗徒劳无功的星辰，“不成报章”；到这首诗，二星已化作隔河相望的一对恋人。诗从织女落笔：素手纤纤，机杼札札，却终日织不成布，泪落如雨。银河又清又浅，相隔能有多远？偏偏“盈盈一水间，脉脉不得语”。十句中有六句用叠字，音节回环，情致缠绵。钟嵘《诗品》称《古诗》“惊心动魄，可谓几乎一字千金”。
