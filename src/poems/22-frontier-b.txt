== 白雪歌送武判官归京 | Song of White Snow: Seeing Judge Wu Off to the Capital
by: 岑参
form: 七古
tags: 边塞 雪 离别 冬 友情
scene: winter snow mountains fort flag rider
北风卷地白草折，胡天八月即飞雪。
忽如一夜春风来，千树万树梨花开。
散入珠帘湿罗幕，狐裘不暖锦衾薄。
将军角弓不得控，都护铁衣冷难着。
瀚海阑干百丈冰，愁云惨淡万里凝。
中军置酒饮归客，胡琴琵琶与羌笛。
纷纷暮雪下辕门，风掣红旗冻不翻。
轮台东门送君去，去时雪满天山路。
山回路转不见君，雪上空留马行处。
--
The north wind rolls the land and snaps the white grass low;
In the eighth month, the northern sky is flying thick with snow.
As if, in a single night, the breeze of spring had come,
A thousand trees, ten thousand trees, burst into pear-blossom.
It drifts through pearl curtains, soaks the silken tent within;
Fox furs give no warmth, and brocade quilts seem thin.
The general cannot draw his horn-bow, stiff with cold;
The Protector's iron armour is too chill to hold.
The desert sea is cracked with ice a hundred fathoms high;
Gloomy clouds, ten thousand li, are frozen as they lie.
In the central tent we set out wine to toast the parting guest,
With Hu fiddles, pipas and Qiang flutes playing their best.
At dusk the snow falls thick and fast upon the camp's front gate;
The red flag, frozen stiff, won't flutter in the wind's weight.
By Luntai's eastern gate I see you on your way;
Snow fills the road across the Tianshan as you leave today.
The mountain bends, the road turns, and you're lost from view;
Only hoofprints in the snow remain, where your horse went through.
--
Luntai, in today's Xinjiang, 754: Cen Shen sees off a colleague returning to Chang'an. Snow arriving overnight like pear blossom on a spring wind is the most famous snow image in Chinese poetry. The ending — the friend gone, only hoofprints left in the snow — is pure longing.
--
天宝十三载（754）起，岑参第二次出塞，在安西北庭节度使封常清幕府任判官，此诗即在轮台（今新疆境内）送同僚武判官回长安时所作。胡地八月已飞雪，诗人却说是一夜春风吹开了千树万树梨花，以春写冬，奇丽温暖，成为咏雪的千古名句。中间极写奇寒：狐裘不暖，角弓难拉，红旗冻得风也吹不动。结尾山回路转，友人已不见，雪地上只留下一行马蹄印，余味悠长。

== 逢入京使 | Meeting an Envoy Bound for the Capital
by: 岑参
form: 七绝
tags: 思乡 边塞 羁旅
scene: day desert rider horse two
故园东望路漫漫，双袖龙钟泪不干。
马上相逢无纸笔，凭君传语报平安。
--
I gaze east toward my home — the road goes on and on;
Both my sleeves are soaked with tears that never dry.
We meet on horseback — no brush, no paper, none;
Please tell my family I'm safe and well, as you pass by.
--
On his way to the Central Asian frontier in 749, Cen Shen meets an envoy heading back to Chang'an. No time to write — only a spoken message: "I'm safe." The simplest words carry the deepest feeling.
--
天宝八载（749），岑参入安西节度使高仙芝幕府，第一次远赴西域，此诗就写在西行途中。回望东边的故园，长路漫漫，泪水沾湿了双袖；路上偶遇回京的使者，马上相逢，来不及写信，只能托他捎一句口信：我平安。千言万语化作“报平安”三个字，朴素至极，却最能打动人心。（“龙钟”在这里是沾湿、淋漓的样子。）

== 别董大（其一） | Farewell to Dong the Elder (I)
by: 高适
form: 七绝
tags: 离别 友情 冬 雪 鸟
scene: winter snow geese clouds sun two rider
千里黄云白日曛，北风吹雁雪纷纷。
莫愁前路无知己，天下谁人不识君。
--
A thousand li of yellow cloud; the white sun's dim and low;
The north wind blows the wild geese on through swirling snow.
Don't fear the road ahead will hold no friend who knows your name —
Who in all the world is there who doesn't know your fame?
--
Dong the Elder was Dong Tinglan, the greatest qin player of the age. Gao Shi, then poor and unknown, sees him off in a snowstorm with no gift but this encouragement — one of the most heartening farewell poems ever written.
--
一般认为作于天宝六载（747）冬，高适与董大在睢阳（今河南商丘）相会后分别。董大多认为是名琴师董庭兰，在家中排行第一（敦煌写本题作《别董令望》，身份尚有异说）。当时高适仕途未达，生活困顿，第二首就说“今日相逢无酒钱”。前两句黄云蔽日、北风送雁、大雪纷飞，一片苍茫；后两句却振起精神，劝友人不必担心前路没有知己，天下谁人不认识你？送别诗一扫儿女沾巾之态，豪迈而温暖。

== 燕歌行 | Song of Yan
by: 高适
form: 七古
tags: 边塞 战争 民生 相思
scene: dusk desert fort flag rider mountains
汉家烟尘在东北，汉将辞家破残贼。
男儿本自重横行，天子非常赐颜色。
摐金伐鼓下榆关，旌旆逶迤碣石间。
校尉羽书飞瀚海，单于猎火照狼山。
山川萧条极边土，胡骑凭陵杂风雨。
战士军前半死生，美人帐下犹歌舞。
大漠穷秋塞草腓，孤城落日斗兵稀。
身当恩遇恒轻敌，力尽关山未解围。
铁衣远戍辛勤久，玉箸应啼别离后。
少妇城南欲断肠，征人蓟北空回首。
边庭飘飖那可度，绝域苍茫更何有。
杀气三时作阵云，寒声一夜传刁斗。
相看白刃血纷纷，死节从来岂顾勋。
君不见沙场征战苦，至今犹忆李将军。
--
Smoke and dust of war rise in the Han's north-east;
Han generals leave their homes to crush the rebels, to the least.
Men of spirit prize the chance to ride across the land;
The Son of Heaven grants them favour, rare and grand.
With clanging gongs and pounding drums they march down through Elm Pass;
Their banners wind among the crags of Jieshi, mass on mass.
The colonels' feathered war-reports fly over the desert sea;
On Wolf Mountain the khan's hunting fires blaze free.
Mountains and rivers are desolate to the edge of the frontier ground;
Hu horsemen charge with wind and rain, swarming all around.
At the front, the soldiers — half are dead and half alive;
Beneath the general's tent, the beauties sing and dance and thrive.
In the great desert, autumn's end; the frontier grass is sere;
The lone fort at sunset — few soldiers left to fight are here.
So favoured by the emperor, the general scorned the foe;
Now, strength spent at the passes, the siege won't lift or go.
In iron mail on distant guard through long and bitter years —
Since parting, their wives must weep their jade-chopstick tears.
South of the city, young wives' hearts are breaking with despair;
North in Ji, the soldiers turn their heads in vain, to stare.
The frontier's wild and windswept — how can it be crossed?
The far land's vast and empty — nothing there but loss.
All day the killing-air forms battle clouds on high;
All night the watchmen's gongs ring out their freezing cry.
They face each other's naked blades; the blood in spatters flies —
To die for duty, who would think of honours or of prize?
Have you not seen the bitterness of fighting on the sands?
To this day the soldiers long for General Li to lead their bands.
--
Written in 738 after news of a disastrous campaign in the north-east, where a general's arrogance and false victory reports cost many lives. "At the front, half the soldiers dead; in the general's tent, beauties still singing and dancing" is the most damning couplet in frontier poetry. "Jade chopsticks" are a woman's two streams of tears. General Li is Li Guang of the Han, who shared every hardship with his men.
--
诗前有序：开元二十六年（738），有人随御史大夫张公（幽州节度使张守珪）出塞归来，写了一首《燕歌行》给高适看，高适有感于征戍之事，作此和之。据史书记载，这一年张守珪的部将出击失利，他却隐瞒败状、谎报战功。诗中将帅受恩轻敌、士卒力尽被围，“战士军前半死生，美人帐下犹歌舞”一联对比鲜明，是边塞诗中最尖锐的讽刺。“玉箸”喻思妇的两行泪水。结尾怀念体恤士卒的“李将军”，一般认为指汉代名将李广，一说指战国赵将李牧。此诗被推为高适边塞诗的代表作。

== 古从军行 | An Old Army Song
by: 李颀
form: 七古
tags: 边塞 战争 民生 音乐
scene: dusk desert snow fort horse geese
白日登山望烽火，黄昏饮马傍交河。
行人刁斗风沙暗，公主琵琶幽怨多。
野云万里无城郭，雨雪纷纷连大漠。
胡雁哀鸣夜夜飞，胡儿眼泪双双落。
闻道玉门犹被遮，应将性命逐轻车。
年年战骨埋荒外，空见蒲桃入汉家。
--
By day we climb the hills to watch for beacon fires;
At dusk we water horses by the Jiao's cold tide.
The watch-gongs clang through the dark of wind and sand;
The princess's pipa sings of grief she cannot hide.
Wild clouds for ten thousand li, and not a town or wall;
Rain and snow come swirling down across the desert wide.
The northern geese cry mournfully, night after night they fly;
The Hu boys' tears fall down in pairs, and none can dry.
They say the Jade Gate's still shut against our coming home —
We must follow the light chariots, and stake our lives, and die.
Year after year, the bones of war are buried in the waste —
And all we get is grapes, brought into the Han to taste.
--
An "old" frontier song, set in the Han but aimed at Xuanzong's wars. The princess's pipa recalls the Han princess married off to a Central Asian king, who played her grief on the pipa. When Emperor Wu of Han's army failed against Ferghana, he shut the Jade Gate and forbade the soldiers to return; the only gain from all the dead was the grapevine, brought back to China. The last line is one of the bitterest in Tang poetry.
--
题为“古”，写的是汉事，讽的却是唐玄宗时连年开边。白天登山望烽火，黄昏饮马交河边，风沙里只有刁斗声，又仿佛听见当年远嫁乌孙的汉家公主弹起幽怨的琵琶。连胡雁、胡儿都在悲啼，戍卒的苦更不待言。“玉门犹被遮”用汉武帝的故事：李广利远征大宛失利，请求退兵，武帝派人封住玉门关，敢入关者斩，士兵只能拼死向前。结句说年年白骨埋在荒野，换来的不过是葡萄传入中原，冷冷一句，讽意极深。（“蒲桃”即葡萄。）

== 塞下曲（其二） | Frontier Song (II)
by: 卢纶
form: 五绝
tags: 边塞 壮志 夜
scene: night crescent pine grass wind rider
林暗草惊风，将军夜引弓。
平明寻白羽，没在石棱中。
--
Dark woods; the grass starts up as wind sweeps by;
The general draws his bow at dead of night.
At dawn they seek his white-fletched arrow — why,
It's buried in a rock, sunk out of sight!
--
Based on a story of the Han general Li Guang, who shot at what he took for a tiger in the dark grass — and in the morning found his arrow buried in a rock. The "grass startled by wind" hints at the tiger without naming it.
--
原题《和张仆射塞下曲》，共六首，是卢纶与一位张姓仆射的唱和之作。此首化用《史记》中李广的故事：李广出猎，见草中有石，以为是虎，一箭射去，箭头竟没入石中。诗人写林间昏暗，草木忽被风吹动，仿佛有猛兽潜伏，将军当即开弓；天亮去寻那支白羽箭，它竟深深嵌进了石棱里。通篇不着一个“虎”字，也不直说将军如何勇武，只凭一个结果，便写尽了他的神力。

== 塞下曲（其三） | Frontier Song (III)
by: 卢纶
form: 五绝
tags: 边塞 雪 冬 夜 壮志
scene: night winter snow geese rider horse
月黑雁飞高，单于夜遁逃。
欲将轻骑逐，大雪满弓刀。
--
No moon; the wild geese fly up high and far;
The khan has fled, escaping in the night.
We'd chase him down with our light horse —
Heavy snow covers bows and blades in white.
--
The night is so dark that the khan's escape is known only from the startled geese. The last line — the cavalry poised, snow piling on their weapons — freezes the moment before the chase.
--
《和张仆射塞下曲》六首中的第三首。没有月光的黑夜，雁群高高惊飞，原来是单于趁着夜色悄悄逃走了。将军正要率领轻骑追击，大雪纷纷而下，转眼落满了弓和刀。诗到这里戛然而止，追击的结果一字不写，只留下雪夜里整装待发的一幕，边塞的严寒与将士的豪气都在其中。（单于是匈奴君主的称号，这里泛指敌军首领。）

== 夜上受降城闻笛 | Hearing a Flute at Night on the Wall of Shouxiang
by: 李益
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 思乡 月 夜 音乐
scene: night moon desert fort frost
回乐烽前沙似雪，受降城外月如霜。
不知何处吹芦管，一夜征人尽望乡。
--
Before Huile's beacon tower, the sand lies white as snow;
Outside the Surrender City walls, the moon is like the frost.
From somewhere — who knows where? — a reed pipe's playing low;
All night the soldiers gaze toward home, and feel what they have lost.
--
Shouxiang ("Accepting Surrender") was a fortress on the Yellow River in the far north. Sand and moonlight turn the world white; an unseen reed pipe plays; every soldier turns toward home. Set to music, the poem was sung all over the empire.
--
李益久在北方边地幕府任职，以边塞诗著名。回乐是唐代灵州属县，在今宁夏灵武一带；受降城是唐代为接受、防御北方部族而筑的边城（具体指哪一座，说法不一）。烽火台前的沙地白得像雪，城外的月光冷得像霜，一片清寒。忽然不知何处吹起芦管，悠悠一夜，所有出征的人都望向故乡。笛声只写一句，思乡只写一句，却写出了千万戍卒共同的心事。据《旧唐书》记载，李益每作一诗，宫廷乐工便争着重金求取，谱成歌曲供奉。

== 陇西行（其二） | Song of Longxi (II)
by: 陈陶
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 战争 悼亡 相思
scene: dusk desert river grass fort
誓扫匈奴不顾身，五千貂锦丧胡尘。
可怜无定河边骨，犹是春闺梦里人。
--
They swore to sweep the Xiongnu out, with no thought for their life;
Five thousand men in sable and brocade fell in the northern dust.
Alas — the bones along the Wuding River, white and bare,
Are still the living men in the spring dreams of each wife.
--
The most heartbreaking war poem in Chinese: the wives at home, not yet knowing, still dream of their husbands — who are already bones by the Wuding River ("River of No Fixed Course") on the northern frontier. "Sable and brocade" was the uniform of elite Han troops.
--
陈陶是晚唐诗人，晚年隐居洪州西山。前两句写将士誓扫匈奴、奋不顾身，五千身穿锦衣貂裘的精兵却葬身胡地风尘（“貂锦”借指精锐部队）。后两句是全诗的精华：无定河边早已是累累白骨，闺中的妻子不知噩耗，仍在梦里与他相会。一边是“骨”，一边是“梦里人”，现实与梦境相隔，悲痛倍增。这首诗选入《唐诗三百首》，“可怜无定河边骨，犹是春闺梦里人”成为控诉战争最沉痛的名句之一。（无定河在今陕西北部，因流沙淤积、河道无定而得名。）
