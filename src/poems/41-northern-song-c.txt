== 山园小梅（其一） | Little Plum Tree in the Mountain Garden (I)
by: 林逋
form: 七律
tags: 花 冬 隐逸 月 咏物
scene: dusk winter moon plum lake crane
众芳摇落独暄妍，占尽风情向小园。
疏影横斜水清浅，暗香浮动月黄昏。
霜禽欲下先偷眼，粉蝶如知合断魂。
幸有微吟可相狎，不须檀板共金尊。
--
When all the flowers have fallen, it alone is fresh and fair,
And claims the charm of my small garden as its own.
Its sparse shadows lie aslant on water clear and shallow;
Its hidden fragrance drifts in the dusk where the moon has shone.
The frost-white birds, about to land, first steal a glance;
The powdered butterflies, if they knew, would faint, every one.
Thank heaven for quiet chanting, to keep it company —
No need for singers' clappers or gold wine cups, none.
--
Lin Bu, the hermit of West Lake, "took plum blossom for his wife and cranes for his children". 疏影横斜水清浅，暗香浮动月黄昏 — "sparse shadows slanting over clear, shallow water; a hidden fragrance floating in the moonlit dusk" — became the definitive description of plum blossom; Jiang Kui named two of his finest lyrics "Hidden Fragrance" and "Sparse Shadows".

== 临江仙（梦后楼台高锁） | Immortal by the River (After the Dream, the Tower Is Locked)
by: 晏几道
form: 词
tags: 相思 爱情 春 花 鸟 音乐
scene: spring rain petals swallows tower moon person
梦后楼台高锁，酒醒帘幕低垂。
去年春恨却来时。落花人独立，微雨燕双飞。

记得小蘋初见，两重心字罗衣。
琵琶弦上说相思。当时明月在，曾照彩云归。
--
After the dream, the tower's locked up high;
I wake from wine; the curtains hang down low.
Last spring's sorrow comes back again, as then:
Amid the falling petals, someone stands alone;
In the fine rain, the swallows fly in pairs.

I remember when I first saw Xiaopin,
In her silk dress embroidered with "heart" twice over.
On the pipa strings she told of love's longing.
The same bright moon was shining then
That lit her, a coloured cloud, as she went home.
--
Yan Jidao remembers Xiaopin, a singing girl in a friend's household. 落花人独立，微雨燕双飞 — "among falling petals, someone stands alone; in fine rain, swallows fly in pairs" — was borrowed from an obscure earlier poem, but made immortal here. The "coloured cloud" is Xiaopin herself, lit home by a moon that still shines though she is gone.

== 鹧鸪天（彩袖殷勤捧玉钟） | Partridge Sky (Your Coloured Sleeves Offered the Jade Cup)
by: 晏几道
form: 词
tags: 爱情 相思 饮酒 夜 音乐
scene: night moon tower willow candle two cup
彩袖殷勤捧玉钟，当年拚却醉颜红。
舞低杨柳楼心月，歌尽桃花扇底风。

从别后，忆相逢，几回魂梦与君同。
今宵剩把银釭照，犹恐相逢是梦中。
--
Your coloured sleeves so kindly offered me the cup of jade;
That year I drank without a care, my face aflame with wine.
We danced until the moon sank low above the willowed tower;
We sang until the breeze was spent from your peach-blossom fan, so fine.

Since we parted,
I've remembered how we met;
How many times my dreaming soul has been with you!
Tonight I keep on lifting up the silver lamp to see —
Afraid our meeting's just a dream, and won't come true.
--
A reunion with a singing girl after years apart. The second couplet — dancing till the moon sank over the willows, singing till the breeze of the peach-blossom fan was spent — is one of the most glittering in the *ci*. Holding up the lamp again and again to make sure it is not a dream echoes Du Fu's reunion with his family: "we face each other as if in a dream."

== 清平乐（春归何处） | Pure Serene Music (Where Has Spring Gone?)
by: 黄庭坚
form: 词
tags: 春 鸟 花 时光
scene: spring oriole flowers wind cottage
春归何处？寂寞无行路。
若有人知春去处，唤取归来同住。

春无踪迹谁知？除非问取黄鹂。
百啭无人能解，因风飞过蔷薇。
--
Where has spring gone home?
It's lonely here — no tracks to trace.
If anyone knows where spring has gone,
Call it back to stay with us in this place.

Spring leaves no trace — who knows?
Unless you ask the oriole.
But no one understands its hundred songs —
It rides the wind across the roses, and goes.
--
A playful chase after the departing spring. The only witness, the oriole, sings a hundred songs no one understands, and flies off over the roses — the flowers of early summer. Spring is gone.
