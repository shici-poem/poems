== 出塞（其一） | Beyond the Frontier (I)
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 战争 月 爱国
scene: night moon fort mountains flag
秦时明月汉时关，万里长征人未还。
但使龙城飞将在，不教胡马度阴山。
--
The moon of Qin, the passes of the Han —
Ten thousand li they marched; not one man has come back.
If only the Flying General of Dragon City were alive,
No Hu horse would cross the Yin Hills to attack.
--
"Qin moon, Han passes": the same moon and the same forts have watched a thousand years of frontier war — the whole of history in one line. The Flying General was Li Guang of the Han, whom the Xiongnu feared above all others. Often called the finest quatrain of the Tang.

== 从军行（其四） | Army Song (IV)
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 战争 壮志 雪
scene: snow mountains clouds fort desert flag
青海长云暗雪山，孤城遥望玉门关。
黄沙百战穿金甲，不破楼兰终不还。
--
Long clouds over Qinghai darken the snowy peaks;
From the lone fort I gaze toward Jade Gate, far away.
A hundred battles in the yellow sand have worn our armour through —
Until we've crushed Loulan, we'll never go home; we'll stay.
--
Qinghai Lake, the snow-capped Qilian range and the Jade Gate Pass trace the whole north-western frontier of the Tang. The soldiers' oath in the last line — 不破楼兰终不还 — is still quoted as a vow of total resolve.

== 从军行（其五） | Army Song (V)
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 战争 壮志
scene: dusk desert wind flag rider fort
大漠风尘日色昏，红旗半卷出辕门。
前军夜战洮河北，已报生擒吐谷浑。
--
Across the great desert, wind and dust have dimmed the sun;
Red banners half-furled, they ride out through the camp's gate.
The vanguard fought by night north of the Tao — and won:
The Tuyuhun chief's been taken alive — the news can't wait!
--
A burst of victory: the reinforcements are still marching into the dust storm when word arrives that the vanguard has already won. The Tuyuhun were a nomadic kingdom around Qinghai.

== 芙蓉楼送辛渐 | Seeing Xin Jian Off at Lotus Tower
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 离别 友情 雨 江河
scene: dawn rain river tower mountains boat
寒雨连江夜入吴，平明送客楚山孤。
洛阳亲友如相问，一片冰心在玉壶。
--
Cold rain spread over the river as night came into Wu;
At dawn I see you off; the hills of Chu stand lone.
If kin and friends in Luoyang ask about me, tell them true:
"A heart of ice in a pot of jade" — that's all I own.
--
Wang Changling, slandered and demoted, sees a friend off to Luoyang from the Lotus Tower at Zhenjiang. 一片冰心在玉壶 — a heart pure as ice in a jade vessel, unchanged by slander — became an idiom for integrity.

== 闺怨 | A Young Wife's Lament
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 闺怨 春 柳 女性 相思
scene: spring willow tower person
闺中少妇不知愁，春日凝妆上翠楼。
忽见陌头杨柳色，悔教夫婿觅封侯。
--
The young wife in her chamber doesn't know what sorrow is;
One spring day, all made up, she climbs the jade-green tower.
She suddenly sees the green of the willows along the road —
And rues the day she sent her man to seek a lordship's power.
--
A masterpiece of the sudden turn. The willows by the road — the trees of parting — make her realise in an instant what she gave up when she encouraged her husband to win a title (封侯) at the frontier.

== 采莲曲（其二） | Lotus-Picking Song (II)
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 夏 女性 花 音乐
scene: summer lotus lake boat person
荷叶罗裙一色裁，芙蓉向脸两边开。
乱入池中看不见，闻歌始觉有人来。
--
Lotus leaves and silken skirt are cut from the same green;
Lotus blooms beside her face unfold on either side.
Lost amid the pond, she cannot now be seen —
Until a song rings out, and you know where she hides.
--
The girl is so like the lotus — green skirt, pink cheeks — that she vanishes among the flowers; only her song gives her away.

== 长信秋词（其三） | Autumn Song of Changxin Palace (III)
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 闺怨 女性 秋 鸟
scene: dawn autumn palace crows person
奉帚平明金殿开，暂将团扇共徘徊。
玉颜不及寒鸦色，犹带昭阳日影来。
--
At dawn, with broom in hand, she waits for the golden hall to open,
And lingers for a while beside her round silk fan.
Her jade-white face is worth less than a crow's black feathers —
For the crows still bring the sunlight of Zhaoyang, as only they can.
--
Lady Ban, a virtuous Han consort, was displaced by the dancer Zhao Feiyan and withdrew to the Changxin Palace to serve the Empress Dowager. She once compared herself to a fan, discarded when autumn comes. Here her beauty is worth less than the black crows — who at least fly from Zhaoyang Palace, where the emperor is, with its sunshine (his favour) on their wings.

== 从军行（其一） | Army Song (I)
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 相思 秋 音乐
scene: dusk autumn tower fort sun wind
烽火城西百尺楼，黄昏独上海风秋。
更吹羌笛关山月，无那金闺万里愁。
--
West of the beacon-fire city stands a hundred-foot tower;
At dusk I climb it alone; autumn wind blows off the sea.
Then a Qiang flute plays "Moon over the Mountain Pass" — what power
Can ease her grief ten thousand li away, in her golden room, for me?
--
A soldier on the tower at dusk hears a flute playing a frontier song of homesickness — and thinks not of his own grief but of his wife's, far away, which he can do nothing to relieve. The "sea" is Qinghai Lake.

== 送柴侍御 | Seeing Off Censor Chai
by: 王昌龄
form: 七绝
tags: 离别 友情 月 山
scene: night moon mountains river boat two
沅水通波接武冈，送君不觉有离伤。
青山一道同云雨，明月何曾是两乡。
--
The Yuan's waves flow straight on to Wugang;
Seeing you off, I hardly feel the parting's pain.
One range of green hills shares the same clouds and rain —
And when was the bright moon in two lands? It shines on both the same.
--
A rare cheerful farewell, written in exile at Longbiao: the same river, the same hills, the same weather and the same moon join the friends, so they are never really apart.

== 登鹳雀楼 | Climbing Stork Tower
by: 王之涣
form: 五绝
tags: 山水 哲理 江河 壮志
scene: dusk sun mountains river tower
白日依山尽，黄河入海流。
欲穷千里目，更上一层楼。
--
The white sun leans on the hills and is gone;
The Yellow River flows on to the sea.
If you would see a thousand li beyond,
Climb one more storey higher — and you'll see.
--
Stork Tower in Puzhou (Shanxi) overlooked the Yellow River. The vast first couplet — the sun vanishing behind mountains, the river rolling to a sea a thousand miles away — leads to the most quoted lines of encouragement in Chinese: to see further, climb higher (更上一层楼), now said of any effort to do better.

== 凉州词（王之涣） | Liangzhou Song (Wang Zhihuan)
by: 王之涣
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 春 思乡 音乐 柳
scene: day clouds mountains fort river desert
黄河远上白云间，一片孤城万仞山。
羌笛何须怨杨柳，春风不度玉门关。
--
Far up, the Yellow River climbs into white cloud;
One lonely fort amid the mountains, ten thousand fathoms tall.
Why must the Qiang flute play "Willows" and mourn aloud?
The spring wind never crosses Jade Gate Pass at all.
--
Liangzhou (Gansu) on the north-western frontier. "Breaking Willows" was the farewell tune — but beyond the Jade Gate there is no spring and there are no willows, so the soldiers' homesickness has nowhere to go. Legend says Wang Zhihuan, Wang Changling and Gao Shi once bet on whose poems the singing girls of a tavern would sing; the loveliest girl sang this one.

== 凉州词（王翰） | Liangzhou Song (Wang Han)
by: 王翰
form: 七绝
tags: 边塞 饮酒 战争 人生
scene: night desert moon cup horse flag
葡萄美酒夜光杯，欲饮琵琶马上催。
醉卧沙场君莫笑，古来征战几人回？
--
Fine wine of grapes in cups of jade that glow at night;
We go to drink — the pipas urge us on from horseback, too.
If I lie drunk upon the sands, don't laugh at the sight:
Since ancient times, of those who went to war, how few came through?
--
Grape wine and "night-shining cups" of white jade were exotic luxuries from Central Asia, and the pipa was the instrument of the steppe. The soldiers drink before battle; the last couplet is both bravado and despair.
