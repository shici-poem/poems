== 滁州西涧 | West Stream at Chuzhou
by: 韦应物
form: 七绝
tags: 春 雨 山水 鸟 闲适
scene: dusk spring rain river boat oriole grass
独怜幽草涧边生，上有黄鹂深树鸣。
春潮带雨晚来急，野渡无人舟自横。
--
I love the hidden grasses growing by the stream,
While orioles sing high up in the deep green trees.
The spring tide, swelled with rain, at evening rushes strong;
At the lonely ferry, no one — the boat turns idly in the breeze.
--
Chuzhou (Anhui), where Wei Yingwu was governor. The last line — an empty ferryboat swinging crosswise in the current — became a favourite subject of painters, and has been read as the poet himself: idle, unused, at peace.

== 秋夜寄邱员外 | On an Autumn Night, to Qiu Dan
by: 韦应物
form: 五绝
tags: 友情 秋 夜 隐逸
scene: night autumn moon pine mountains person
怀君属秋夜，散步咏凉天。
空山松子落，幽人应未眠。
--
On this autumn night I think of you,
And stroll, and chant beneath the cool sky.
In empty hills, the pine cones drop —
And you, the hermit, lie sleepless, as do I.
--
A letter-poem to Qiu Dan, a friend who had become a Daoist recluse. The sound of a pine cone falling in the silence links the two sleepless friends across the distance.

== 淮上喜会梁川故人 | Happy to Meet an Old Friend from Liangchuan on the Huai
by: 韦应物
form: 五律
tags: 友情 时光 秋 饮酒
scene: autumn hills river two cup
江汉曾为客，相逢每醉还。
浮云一别后，流水十年间。
欢笑情如旧，萧疏鬓已斑。
何因不归去？淮上有秋山。
--
Once by the Jiang and Han we roamed as guests;
Whenever we met, we drank, and came home merry.
Then we parted, like the floating clouds;
Ten years have flowed like water, in a hurry.
Our laughter and our love are as of old,
But our thin hair at the temples has turned grey.
You ask me why I don't go home —
The autumn hills along the Huai make me stay.
--
A reunion after ten years. The middle couplet compresses a decade: "a parting like floating clouds, ten years like flowing water". The answer to "why not go home?" — "because there are autumn hills here" — hides behind a love of landscape a reluctance to return home with nothing to show.

== 寄李儋元锡 | To Li Dan and Yuan Xi
by: 韦应物
form: 七律
tags: 友情 春 民生 月 时光
scene: night spring moon tower flowers
去年花里逢君别，今日花开又一年。
世事茫茫难自料，春愁黯黯独成眠。
身多疾病思田里，邑有流亡愧俸钱。
闻道欲来相问讯，西楼望月几回圆。
--
Last year, among the flowers, I met you and we parted;
The flowers bloom today — and one more year has flown.
The world's affairs are vast, and no one can foresee them;
In spring's dark melancholy I fall asleep alone.
My body's full of illness; I long for fields and home;
With refugees in my district, I'm ashamed to draw my pay.
I hear you plan to come and visit me —
From the west tower, how many full moons have I watched, while you delay?
--
Written around 784, while Wei Yingwu governed Chuzhou in the troubled years after the great rebellions. 邑有流亡愧俸钱 — "with refugees in my district, I am ashamed of my salary" — was praised for a thousand years as the conscience of the ideal official.

== 喜外弟卢纶见宿 | Glad That My Cousin Lu Lun Stays the Night
by: 司空曙
form: 五律
tags: 友情 秋 雨 时光 夜
scene: night autumn rain maple leaves cottage candle two
静夜四无邻，荒居旧业贫。
雨中黄叶树，灯下白头人。
以我独沉久，愧君相见频。
平生自有分，况是蔡家亲。
--
A quiet night, and no neighbours near;
In my bare house, my old estate is poor.
In the rain, a tree of yellow leaves;
Beneath the lamp, a white-haired man, and nothing more.
I've long been sunk in solitude, alone —
I'm ashamed you come so often to my door.
Each has his share in life; and more than that,
We're kin, like Cai and Yang, as those before.
--
The poet Lu Lun, Sikong Shu's cousin, comes to stay. 雨中黄叶树，灯下白头人 — "in the rain, a tree of yellow leaves; under the lamp, a white-haired man" — is one of the most admired couplets of the Tang: the old man and the autumn tree mirror each other. The Cai and Yang families of the Jin dynasty were joined by marriage, as the two poets were.

== 听筝 | Listening to the Zheng
by: 李端
form: 五绝
tags: 音乐 爱情 女性
scene: day palace person flowers window
鸣筝金粟柱，素手玉房前。
欲得周郎顾，时时误拂弦。
--
The zither sings on bridges flecked with gold;
White hands play by the jade room, deft and slow.
To win a glance from Master Zhou, so bold,
She strikes a wrong note now and then — just so.
--
In the Three Kingdoms period, the young general Zhou Yu was such a fine musician that "if a note was wrong, Master Zhou would turn and look". So the clever player misplays on purpose — to make the handsome listener look at her.

== 月夜 | Moonlit Night
by: 刘方平
form: 七绝
tags: 春 夜 月 天空
scene: night spring moon stars cottage window
更深月色半人家，北斗阑干南斗斜。
今夜偏知春气暖，虫声新透绿窗纱。
--
Late at night, the moon lights half the houses' face;
The Northern Dipper slants; the Southern sinks in place.
Tonight I know at last the air of spring is warm —
The insects' songs come newly through the green window's lace.
--
The first insect song of the year, heard through the window screen, tells the poet that spring has come — a discovery made by ear, in the moonlit night.

== 春怨（刘方平） | Spring Lament (Liu Fangping)
by: 刘方平
form: 七绝
tags: 闺怨 春 花 女性
scene: dusk spring petals palace window
纱窗日落渐黄昏，金屋无人见泪痕。
寂寞空庭春欲晚，梨花满地不开门。
--
Past the gauze window the sun goes down; the dusk falls slow;
In her golden chamber, no one sees her tear-stained face.
The empty courtyard's lonely; spring is nearly gone, I know;
Pear blossoms cover the ground; the gate stays shut in place.
--
A forgotten palace lady — "golden chamber" recalls a Han emperor's promise to house his beloved in a house of gold. The unopened gate among fallen pear petals says that no one comes, and that she no longer expects anyone.

== 省试湘灵鼓瑟 | The Spirit of the Xiang Plays Her Zither: An Examination Poem
by: 钱起
form: 排律
tags: 音乐 江河 山 女性
scene: day river peaks mist lake
善鼓云和瑟，常闻帝子灵。
冯夷空自舞，楚客不堪听。
苦调凄金石，清音入杳冥。
苍梧来怨慕，白芷动芳馨。
流水传潇浦，悲风过洞庭。
曲终人不见，江上数峰青。
--
On her cloud-wood zither she plays with skill —
Often we've heard of the river goddess's art.
The River Lord dances, but in vain;
The exile from Chu cannot bear it in his heart.
Her bitter notes make metal and stone grieve;
Her clear tones pierce the dark void far away.
From Cangwu comes the sorrow of her longing;
White angelica stirs its fragrance in the spray.
The flowing water bears it down the Xiao;
A mournful wind sweeps over Dongting's sheen.
The music ends — and no one can be seen;
Upon the river, a few peaks rise, green.
--
Written for the imperial examination of 751 on a set theme: the goddess of the Xiang River — a daughter of the sage-king Yao, who drowned herself in grief when her husband Shun died at Cangwu — playing her zither. Most of the poem is an examination exercise, but the last couplet, as the music ends and the goddess vanishes, leaving only green peaks on the river, is pure magic. Legend says Qian Qi heard those lines spoken by a ghost.

== 春怨（金昌绪） | Spring Lament (Jin Changxu)
by: 金昌绪
form: 五绝
tags: 闺怨 相思 春 鸟 女性
scene: dawn spring willow oriole cottage person
打起黄莺儿，莫教枝上啼。
啼时惊妾梦，不得到辽西。
--
Go, shoo that oriole away!
Don't let it sing upon the bough —
Its singing woke me from my dream
Before I could reach Liaoxi, somehow.
--
A young wife chases a singing oriole out of the tree — because it woke her just as, in her dream, she was about to reach her husband at the frontier in Liaoxi, in the far north-east. The whole story unfolds backwards, one line at a time.
