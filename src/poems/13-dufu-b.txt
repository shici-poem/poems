== 兵车行 | Ballad of the Army Carts
by: 杜甫
form: 乐府
tags: 战争 民生 边塞
scene: day bridge wall rider horse flag wind
车辚辚，马萧萧，行人弓箭各在腰。
耶娘妻子走相送，尘埃不见咸阳桥。
牵衣顿足拦道哭，哭声直上干云霄。
道旁过者问行人，行人但云点行频。
或从十五北防河，便至四十西营田。
去时里正与裹头，归来头白还戍边。
边庭流血成海水，武皇开边意未已。
君不闻汉家山东二百州，千村万落生荆杞。
纵有健妇把锄犁，禾生陇亩无东西。
况复秦兵耐苦战，被驱不异犬与鸡。
长者虽有问，役夫敢申恨？
且如今年冬，未休关西卒。
县官急索租，租税从何出？
信知生男恶，反是生女好。
生女犹得嫁比邻，生男埋没随百草。
君不见青海头，古来白骨无人收。
新鬼烦冤旧鬼哭，天阴雨湿声啾啾！
--
The chariots rumble, the horses neigh,
Bows and arrows at each marcher's waist on the way.
Fathers, mothers, wives and children run to see them go;
The dust is so thick that Xianyang Bridge no longer shows.
They clutch at clothes, they stamp, they block the road and cry;
The sound of weeping rises straight up to the sky.
A passer-by beside the road asks one of the men;
The man just says, "They call us up again and again.
Some went north at fifteen to guard the Yellow River's shore;
At forty they were sent out west to farm the army's store.
When they left, the village head bound up their hair;
They came home white — and now they're sent back to the frontier.
At the border, blood has flowed until it formed a sea,
But the Martial Emperor still wants more land — he won't let be.
Have you not heard? In Han's two hundred districts east of the hills,
Ten thousand villages lie choked with thorns that no one tills.
Even where strong wives take the hoe and plough in hand,
The crops grow wild and tangled, every which way, on the land.
And since the men of Qin are tough and can endure the fight,
They're driven out like dogs and chickens, day and night.
Though you, sir, ask me this,
How dare a conscript tell you what's amiss?
Take this winter — even now
They haven't sent the western troops back to the plough.
The county officers press hard for tax that we can't pay —
Where is it to come from? Tell me, sir, I pray.
Truly, it's bad luck to have a son, we've learned;
Far better to have a daughter — the tables have turned.
A girl can still be married to the neighbours next door;
A boy lies buried in the grass, and is seen no more.
Have you not seen, beside the Qinghai Lake,
White bones from ancient times that no one comes to take?
New ghosts rage at their wrongs, and the old ghosts wail;
When skies are dark and the rain is wet, you hear them cry in the gale."
--
About 751, a protest against Emperor Xuanzong's endless wars of conquest. Du Fu disguises the present emperor as "the Martial Emperor" — Emperor Wu of Han — and lets a conscript speak. The reversal of the age-old preference for sons ("a son is buried in the grass") was shocking. Xianyang Bridge crossed the Wei River west of Chang'an, where the troops set out; Qinghai Lake was a killing ground in the wars with Tibet.

== 前出塞（其六） | Going Out to the Frontier (VI)
by: 杜甫
form: 五古
tags: 战争 边塞 哲理
scene: day fort flag rider mountains
挽弓当挽强，用箭当用长。
射人先射马，擒贼先擒王。
杀人亦有限，列国自有疆。
苟能制侵陵，岂在多杀伤。
--
To draw a bow, then draw a strong one;
To use an arrow, use a long one.
To shoot a man, first shoot his mount;
To catch the bandits, catch their king — that counts.
But killing, too, should have its limit,
And every land has borders — stay within it.
If we can halt the invaders' aggression,
What need for slaughter past all discretion?
--
From a series of nine poems in the voice of a soldier on Xuanzong's campaigns. The first four lines sound like military maxims ("to catch bandits, first catch their king" became a proverb); then the soldier turns: war should be for defence, not conquest.

== 赠卫八处士 | For the Recluse Wei the Eighth
by: 杜甫
form: 五古
tags: 友情 时光 人生 夜 雨
scene: night rain cottage candle two jar
人生不相见，动如参与商。
今夕复何夕，共此灯烛光。
少壮能几时，鬓发各已苍。
访旧半为鬼，惊呼热中肠。
焉知二十载，重上君子堂。
昔别君未婚，儿女忽成行。
怡然敬父执，问我来何方。
问答乃未已，驱儿罗酒浆。
夜雨剪春韭，新炊间黄粱。
主称会面难，一举累十觞。
十觞亦不醉，感子故意长。
明日隔山岳，世事两茫茫。
--
In this life, old friends so seldom meet —
Like Shen and Shang, two stars that never share the sky.
What night is this night, then, so sweet,
That we two share this candle's light, you and I?
How long can youth and vigour stay?
Already both our temples have turned grey.
I ask for old friends — half are ghosts by now;
I cry out, and my heart burns, I don't know how.
Who'd think that twenty years would go
Before I climbed into your hall again?
When last we parted, you'd no wife, I know;
Now sons and daughters stand in line — and then
They greet their father's friend with smiling grace
And ask me what far place I've journeyed from.
Before our questions and replies have run their race,
You send them off: "Bring wine, and quickly come!"
Spring chives are cut out in the night rain;
New-cooked rice is mixed with yellow grain.
"It's hard for us to meet," my host declares,
And drinks ten cups to me in one long go;
Ten cups — and still I'm sober: unawares,
Your old affection moves me so.
Tomorrow mountains rise between us two,
And the world's affairs will be lost from view.
--
759, a time of war: Du Fu spends a night with an old friend, a scholar who never took office. Its power lies in homely detail — the children politely asking where he has come from, spring chives cut in the rain. Shen (Orion) and Shang (Antares) lie on opposite sides of the sky: when one rises, the other has set.

== 戏为六绝句（其二） | Six Playful Quatrains (II)
by: 杜甫
form: 七绝
tags: 哲理 时光 江河
scene: day river mountains clouds
王杨卢骆当时体，轻薄为文哂未休。
尔曹身与名俱灭，不废江河万古流。
--
Wang, Yang, Lu, Luo — they wrote in their own day's style;
Glib critics mock their work and never cease to jeer.
But you will perish, body and name, in a little while,
While they, like the great rivers, flow forever here.
--
One of six playful quatrains on literary criticism. The "Four Paragons of the Early Tang" — Wang Bo, Yang Jiong, Lu Zhaolin and Luo Binwang — were sneered at by later critics as old-fashioned. Du Fu's retort is now quoted for any achievement that outlasts its critics.

== 江村 | River Village
by: 杜甫
form: 七律
tags: 田园 闲适 夏 江河
scene: summer river cottage swallows birds two
清江一曲抱村流，长夏江村事事幽。
自去自来梁上燕，相亲相近水中鸥。
老妻画纸为棋局，稚子敲针作钓钩。
但有故人供禄米，微躯此外更何求？
--
A bend of the clear river hugs the village as it flows;
Long summer days in the river village pass in quiet ease.
The swallows on the rafters come and go just as they choose;
The gulls upon the water draw together as they please.
My old wife draws a chessboard on a paper sheet;
My little son is hammering a needle for a hook.
With an old friend to send me rice, my life's complete —
What more could this frail body seek, or where else look?
--
Chengdu, summer 760, at the newly built thatched cottage. After years of war and wandering, Du Fu enjoys a rare peace: swallows and gulls, his wife's homemade chessboard, his son's homemade fish-hook. The rice came from his patron and friend Yan Wu.

== 饮中八仙歌 | Song of the Eight Immortals of the Wine Cup
by: 杜甫
form: 七古
tags: 饮酒 人生 友情
scene: day palace cup jar rider
知章骑马似乘船，眼花落井水底眠。
汝阳三斗始朝天，道逢麴车口流涎，恨不移封向酒泉。
左相日兴费万钱，饮如长鲸吸百川，衔杯乐圣称避贤。
宗之潇洒美少年，举觞白眼望青天，皎如玉树临风前。
苏晋长斋绣佛前，醉中往往爱逃禅。
李白一斗诗百篇，长安市上酒家眠。
天子呼来不上船，自称臣是酒中仙。
张旭三杯草圣传，脱帽露顶王公前，挥毫落纸如云烟。
焦遂五斗方卓然，高谈雄辩惊四筵。
--
He Zhizhang sways on horseback as if he's sailing in a boat;
Eyes swimming, he falls down a well, and sleeps on the bottom, content.
The Prince of Ruyang drinks three pecks before he goes to court;
He drools when he meets a brewer's cart along the way he went,
And wishes his fief were moved to the town of Wine Spring.
The Chancellor of the Left spends ten thousand cash a day,
Drinks like a great whale gulping a hundred rivers away;
Cup at his lips, he "loves the sage and shuns the worthy", so they say.
Cui Zongzhi, a handsome youth, so carefree and so free,
Lifts his goblet, rolls his eyes at the blue sky —
Bright as a jade tree standing in the breeze.
Su Jin keeps a long fast before the embroidered Buddha's shrine,
But drunk, he often likes to slip away from Zen to wine.
Li Bai — one peck of wine, a hundred poems!
He sleeps in taverns in Chang'an's market streets.
When the Son of Heaven summons him, he won't board the royal boat:
"Your servant is an immortal of wine," is what he repeats.
Zhang Xu — three cups, and he's the Sage of Cursive Script, it's said;
Before the princes and the lords he takes his cap off, bares his head;
His brush falls on the paper like clouds and mists outspread.
Jiao Sui needs five pecks before he's at his best;
Then his lofty talk and fierce debate astonish every guest.
--
A comic gallery of eight famous drinkers of Chang'an around 740: the poet He Zhizhang, Prince Li Jin of Ruyang, the chancellor Li Shizhi, the dandy Cui Zongzhi, the devout Su Jin, Li Bai, the calligrapher Zhang Xu and the debater Jiao Sui — each sketched in a few lines. The Li Bai lines made his legend. Wine Spring (Jiuquan) is a real town on the Silk Road; "the sage" and "the worthy" were drinkers' slang for clear and cloudy wine; "white eyes" meant looking down on the vulgar.

== 曲江二首（其二） | Winding River (II)
by: 杜甫
form: 七律
tags: 饮酒 时光 人生 春
scene: spring river flowers butterfly person
朝回日日典春衣，每日江头尽醉归。
酒债寻常行处有，人生七十古来稀。
穿花蛱蝶深深见，点水蜻蜓款款飞。
传语风光共流转，暂时相赏莫相违。
--
Back from court each day, I pawn my clothes of spring,
And every evening by the river come home drunk as can be.
Wine debts are common — I owe them everywhere I go;
A man who lives to seventy has always been rare to see.
Deep among the flowers the butterflies are glimpsed;
Dipping the water, dragonflies drift slow and free.
I send word to the spring: let us drift on together,
And enjoy each other a while — don't turn away from me.
--
Spring 758, at Qujiang ("Winding River"), the pleasure park of Chang'an. Du Fu, holding a minor post at court, pawns his clothes to drink — less from joy than from disillusion. "Since ancient times, seventy has been rare" gave Chinese the word 古稀 for the age of seventy. (Du Fu himself died at fifty-eight.)

== 又呈吴郎 | Again to Mr Wu
by: 杜甫
form: 七律
tags: 民生 战争 人生
scene: autumn cottage two
堂前扑枣任西邻，无食无儿一妇人。
不为困穷宁有此？只缘恐惧转须亲。
即防远客虽多事，便插疏篱却甚真。
已诉征求贫到骨，正思戎马泪盈巾。
--
Let our west neighbour knock the dates down from the tree —
A woman with no food, no children, all alone.
Would she do this if she weren't in poverty?
She's frightened — so be kinder than you've shown.
Her fear of you, a stranger, may be needless, it is true,
But fencing off the tree would make her fears seem right.
She's told me that the levies have bled her to the bone —
I think of war, and tears soak my kerchief through tonight.
--
Du Fu had moved out of his cottage at Kuizhou and lent it to a relative, Mr Wu, who put up a fence to stop a poor widow next door from knocking down dates — as Du Fu had always let her do. This gentle poem-letter asks him to take it down. Few poems anywhere show such quiet, practical compassion.

== 房兵曹胡马 | Officer Fang's Western Horse
by: 杜甫
form: 五律
tags: 壮志 咏物
scene: day horse desert wind
胡马大宛名，锋棱瘦骨成。
竹批双耳峻，风入四蹄轻。
所向无空阔，真堪托死生。
骁腾有如此，万里可横行。
--
A western horse, of the famous Ferghana breed,
All sharp edges, built of bone so lean;
Its ears stand pointed like bamboo sliced for speed;
The wind flies in its hooves — so light, so keen.
Wherever it goes, no space is too wide;
You could trust it with your life and with your death.
With such fierce swiftness at your side,
You could ride ten thousand li in a breath.
--
An early poem (c. 740) on a fine horse belonging to an army officer named Fang. Dayuan (Ferghana, in Central Asia) was famous for its "heavenly horses". The horse is also a portrait of the kind of man the young Du Fu hoped to become.

== 梦李白（其一） | Dreaming of Li Bai (I)
by: 杜甫
form: 五古
tags: 友情 相思 夜 失意
scene: night crescent maple river mountains window
死别已吞声，生别常恻恻。
江南瘴疠地，逐客无消息。
故人入我梦，明我长相忆。
恐非平生魂，路远不可测。
魂来枫林青，魂返关塞黑。
君今在罗网，何以有羽翼？
落月满屋梁，犹疑照颜色。
水深波浪阔，无使蛟龙得。
--
A parting by death — one swallows the sobs at last;
A parting in life brings grief that never ends.
South of the River, the fever lands are vast,
And no news comes from the exile, my friend.
Last night you came into my dream,
To show you know I think of you always.
I fear it was no living soul, it would seem —
The road's too long to fathom, all those days.
Your soul came while the maple woods were green;
Your soul went home through mountain passes black.
You're snared in the net — so how, I mean,
Could you have wings to fly here and fly back?
The setting moon floods all the rafters bright;
I half believe it's shining on your face.
The waters are deep, the waves are wide tonight —
Don't let the dragons drag you from your place!
--
759. Li Bai had been sentenced to exile in Yelang for joining Prince Yong's rebellion, and rumours said he was dead. Du Fu dreams of him and fears the visitor was a ghost. The green maple woods are the south; the black passes, the north where Du Fu lies sleeping. The flood-dragons (蛟龙) are the dangers — and the enemies — on the waters of the south.
