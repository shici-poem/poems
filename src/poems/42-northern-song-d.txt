== 鹊桥仙（纤云弄巧） | Immortals at the Magpie Bridge (Fine Clouds Weave Their Patterns)
by: 秦观
form: 词
tags: 爱情 节日 天空 夜 相思
scene: night stars clouds bridge birds
纤云弄巧，飞星传恨，银汉迢迢暗度。
金风玉露一相逢，便胜却人间无数。

柔情似水，佳期如梦，忍顾鹊桥归路！
两情若是久长时，又岂在朝朝暮暮。
--
Fine clouds weave their clever patterns;
Shooting stars bear sorrow's message far;
Across the Silver River's distance, in secret, she goes.
One meeting in the golden wind and jade-white dew
Is worth more than all the meetings the human world knows.

Their tender love is soft as water;
Their tryst is fleeting as a dream —
How can they bear to look back at the magpie bridge as home they go?
If love between two hearts will last forever,
Why must they be together every dawn and every dusk?
--
On the Seventh Night of the seventh month, the Weaver Girl (Vega) crosses the Silver River (the Milky Way) on a bridge of magpies to meet the Herdboy (Altair) — their one night together each year. Qin Guan turns the usual lament on its head: one meeting of true lovers is worth more than all the ordinary days of humankind, and lasting love does not need to be together "every morning and every evening". Long-distance lovers still quote it.

== 踏莎行·郴州旅舍 | Treading the Grass: At the Inn in Chenzhou
by: 秦观
form: 词
tags: 失意 羁旅 思乡 春 月 鸟
scene: dusk spring mist moon tower river mountains cottage
雾失楼台，月迷津渡，桃源望断无寻处。
可堪孤馆闭春寒，杜鹃声里斜阳暮。

驿寄梅花，鱼传尺素，砌成此恨无重数。
郴江幸自绕郴山，为谁流下潇湘去？
--
The mist has hidden the towers,
The moon has blurred the ford;
I gaze for Peach Blossom Spring — it's nowhere to be found.
How can I bear this lonely inn, shut in the spring cold,
At sunset, with the cuckoo's cries all around?

Plum blossoms sent by post,
And letters in a fish —
They pile up into sorrows, more than can be told.
The Chen River ought to circle its own Chen Hill —
For whom, then, does it flow away to the Xiang, so cold?
--
Written in 1097 in exile at Chenzhou (Hunan) — one of the darkest of all *ci*. Peach Blossom Spring, the hidden paradise, cannot be found; the kind letters and gifts of friends only build his sorrow higher. The last two lines — why does the river leave its own mountains to flow away north? — so moved Su Shi that he wrote them on his fan, saying "even ten thousand men could not redeem Qin Guan".

== 青玉案（凌波不过横塘路） | Green Jade Table (Her Gliding Steps)
by: 贺铸
form: 词
tags: 相思 爱情 春 雨 柳
scene: dusk spring rain river grass willow bridge
凌波不过横塘路，但目送、芳尘去。
锦瑟华年谁与度？月桥花院，琐窗朱户，只有春知处。

飞云冉冉蘅皋暮，彩笔新题断肠句。
若问闲情都几许？一川烟草，满城风絮，梅子黄时雨。
--
Her lightly gliding steps don't come along Hengtang road;
I only watch her fragrant dust drift off.
With whom will she spend the zither-years of youth?
A moonlit bridge, a garden of flowers,
A latticed window, a crimson door —
Only spring knows where.

Slow-drifting clouds; dusk falls on the fragrant shore;
With a coloured brush I write new heartbreaking lines.
You ask how much idle sorrow I have to bear? —
A whole river of misty grass,
A whole city of willow-down in the wind,
And the rain when plums turn yellow everywhere.
--
He Zhu glimpses a beautiful woman — "treading the waves", like the goddess of the Luo River — and never sees her again. His answer to "how much sorrow?" is three images of drifting multitude: misty grass along a whole river, willow-down filling a whole city, and the endless drizzle of the "plum rains" of early summer. It earned him the nickname "He of the Plum Rain".

== 苏幕遮（燎沉香） | Su Mu Zhe (I Burn Aloeswood)
by: 周邦彦
form: 词
tags: 夏 思乡 花 鸟 雨
scene: dawn summer lotus lake birds sun cottage
燎沉香，消溽暑。鸟雀呼晴，侵晓窥檐语。
叶上初阳干宿雨，水面清圆，一一风荷举。

故乡遥，何日去？家住吴门，久作长安旅。
五月渔郎相忆否？小楫轻舟，梦入芙蓉浦。
--
I burn the aloeswood incense
To drive away the muggy heat.
The sparrows call for sunny skies;
At dawn they chatter, peeping from the eaves, so sweet.
On the leaves, the morning sun dries last night's rain;
Across the water, clear and round,
The lotus leaves rise in the wind, one by one, complete.

My home is far away —
When shall I go?
My home is by the gates of Wu;
In the capital so long I've been a guest.
Do the fishermen of the fifth month remember me?
A little oar, a little boat —
In dreams I drift into the lotus pool to rest.
--
Zhou Bangyan, a native of Hangzhou serving in the capital Kaifeng, wakes to a summer morning. 叶上初阳干宿雨，水面清圆，一一风荷举 — "on the leaves the first sun dries the night's rain; on the water, fresh and round, the lotus leaves rise one by one in the wind" — Wang Guowei called the line that "truly captures the spirit of the lotus". From the lotus he drifts into homesickness.

== 卜算子（我住长江头） | Song of Divination (I Live Where the River Starts)
by: 李之仪
form: 词
tags: 爱情 相思 江河
scene: day river boat hills person
我住长江头，君住长江尾。
日日思君不见君，共饮长江水。

此水几时休？此恨何时已？
只愿君心似我心，定不负相思意。
--
I live where the Long River starts;
You live where the Long River ends.
Day after day I long for you, but you're not here —
Yet we both drink the water the same river sends.

When will this river cease to run?
When will this longing ever end?
I only hope your heart is like my own —
Then I'll keep faith with all this love, my friend.
--
A lyric in the plain voice of a folk song. The great Yangtze both separates the lovers — head and tail, a thousand miles apart — and joins them, for they drink the same water. Its simplicity has made it one of the best-loved love poems in Chinese.

== 三衢道中 | On the Road Through Sanqu
by: 曾几
form: 七绝
tags: 夏 山水 鸟 闲适
scene: summer sun mountains river boat oriole pine
梅子黄时日日晴，小溪泛尽却山行。
绿阴不减来时路，添得黄鹂四五声。
--
In plum-yellow season, day after day is fair;
I've sailed the stream to its end, and now I take the mountain way.
The green shade's just as thick as on the road I came,
And four or five oriole songs are added on today.
--
Early summer, the "plum-yellow" season, which is normally rainy — but every day is sunny. A happy little travel poem near Quzhou in Zhejiang: the same green shade going back as coming, with birdsong added.
