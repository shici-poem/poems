孟浩然
Meng Haoran
唐 · Tang · 689–740 · 5 poems

The great landscape poet of the High Tang who never held office, living as a recluse near Xiangyang. Friend of Li Bai and Wang Wei, whose name is always paired with his ("Wang–Meng").
