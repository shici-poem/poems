詩經
The Book of Songs
先秦 · Pre-Qin · c. 1000–600 BC · 11 poems

China's oldest anthology of poetry: 305 songs from the Western Zhou to the Spring and Autumn period, gathered — tradition says — by Confucius. Folk songs, court odes and temple hymns, mostly in four-character lines, with the refrains and nature images that shaped all later Chinese verse.
