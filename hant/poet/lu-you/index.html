陸游
Lu You
宋 · Song · 1125–1210 · 12 poems

The most prolific of all Chinese poets (nearly 10,000 surviving poems), a patriot who longed all his long life to see the north recovered. His one great love, Tang Wan, was taken from him by his mother.
