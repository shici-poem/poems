杜甫
Du Fu
唐 · Tang · 712–770 · 26 poems

The "Sage of Poetry". Lived through the An Lushan rebellion that shattered the Tang, and recorded it — war, famine, refugees, his own poverty — with unmatched compassion and craft. His poems are called "poetic history".
