劉禹錫
Liu Yuxi
唐 · Tang · 772–842 · 10 poems

Called the "Poet Hero". A reformer exiled for over twenty years who never lost his wit or defiance; he adapted southern folk songs into his "Bamboo Branch" lyrics.
