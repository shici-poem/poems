李清照
Li Qingzhao
宋 · Song · 1084–c. 1155 · 9 poems

The greatest woman poet in Chinese. Her early lyrics are bright and playful; after the Jurchen invasion and her husband's death, she wrote some of the most heartbroken poems in the language.
