李煜
Li Yu
五代 · Five Dynasties · 937–978 · 7 poems

Last ruler of the Southern Tang, captured by the Song in 975 and poisoned three years later. His lyrics of lost kingdom and endless sorrow made him the "Emperor of Ci".
