寒夜

〔宋〕杜耒

寒夜客來茶當酒，竹爐湯沸火初紅。

尋常一樣窗前月，才有梅花便不同。

英译 English translation

A Cold Night

Hán Yè
Du Lei · Song · Seven-character quatrain

A guest arrives on a cold night; I serve him tea for wine;

In the bamboo stove the water boils; the fire has just turned red.

The same old moon that always shines outside the window —

But tonight, with plum blossom, it's a different moon instead.

About this poem

The simplest hospitality: tea instead of wine on a winter night. The moon is the same as ever — but the plum blossom, and the friend, make it new. 寻常一样窗前月，才有梅花便不同 is quoted for how a small touch, or a friend, transforms the ordinary.
