贈劉景文

〔宋〕蘇軾

荷盡已無擎雨蓋，菊殘猶有傲霜枝。

一年好景君須記，最是橙黃橘綠時。

英译 English translation

For Liu Jingwen

Zèng Liú Jǐng Wén
Su Shi · Song · Seven-character quatrain

The lotus is done — no leaves left to shelter from the rain;

The fading chrysanthemums still hold up branches frost can't bend.

The finest season of the year — remember it again:

When oranges turn gold and tangerines are green, my friend.

About this poem

Hangzhou, 1090, for Liu Jingwen, a fifty-eight-year-old officer of integrity whom Su Shi admired. Late autumn, when lotus and chrysanthemums fade, is not an ending but the best season of all — the time of ripe fruit: a compliment and an encouragement for a man in later life. The chrysanthemum that "defies the frost" is Liu himself.
