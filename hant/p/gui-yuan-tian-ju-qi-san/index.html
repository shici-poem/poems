歸園田居（其三）

〔魏晉〕陶淵明

種豆南山下，草盛豆苗稀，

晨興理荒穢，帶月荷鋤歸。

道狹草木長，夕露沾我衣，

衣沾不足惜，但使願無違。

英译 English translation

Returning to the Fields (III)

Guī Yuán Tián Jū Qí Sān
Tao Yuanming · Wei & Jin · Five-character old-style verse

I plant my beans below the Southern Hill;

The weeds grow thick, the bean sprouts thin and few.

At dawn I rise to clear away the weeds;

By moonlight I come home, hoe on shoulder, too.

The path is narrow, the grass and bushes tall;

The evening dew has soaked my clothes all through.

Wet clothes are nothing I'd regret —

As long as what I hope for can come true.

About this poem

The poet-farmer is not much of a farmer — the weeds outgrow his beans — but he works from dawn till moonrise, and a damp coat is a small price for living by his own wishes. 带月荷锄归, "coming home with the moon, a hoe on my shoulder", is one of the best-loved images of the simple life.
