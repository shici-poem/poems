蟬

〔唐〕虞世南

垂緌飲清露，流響出疏桐。

居高聲自遠，非是藉秋風。

英译 English translation

The Cicada

Chán
Yu Shinan · Tang · Five-character quatrain

With tasselled mouth it sips the clear, pure dew;

Its flowing song pours from the wutong, sparse and tall.

Its voice goes far because it dwells up high —

And not because the autumn wind has carried it at all.

About this poem

The cicada's mouthparts look like the tassels of an official's cap, and it was believed to live on dew — so it is an upright official. A person of real worth is heard far and wide by his own merit, without needing powerful backing. One of the "three great cicada poems" of the Tang, with Luo Binwang's and Li Shangyin's.
