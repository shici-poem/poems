雪梅（其一）

〔宋〕盧梅坡

梅雪爭春未肯降，騷人閣筆費評章。

梅須遜雪三分白，雪卻輸梅一段香。

英译 English translation

Snow and Plum (I)

Xuě Méi Qí Yī
Lu Meipo · Song · Seven-character quatrain

The plum and snow contend for spring, and neither will give way;

The poet sets his brush aside — he can't decide who's won.

The plum must yield the snow some measure of its white;

The snow must yield the plum its scent — and so the contest's done.

About this poem

A playful debate between two winter beauties. The verdict — snow is whiter, plum is sweeter — became a saying about everyone having their own strengths.
