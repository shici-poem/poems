江城子·乙卯正月二十日夜記夢

〔宋〕蘇軾

十年生死兩茫茫，不思量，自難忘。

千里孤墳，無處話淒涼。

縱使相逢應不識，塵滿面，鬢如霜。

夜來幽夢忽還鄉，小軒窗，正梳妝。

相顧無言，惟有淚千行。

料得年年腸斷處，明月夜，短松岡。

英译 English translation

River Town: A Dream on the Night of the 20th Day of the First Month, 1075

Jiāng Chéng Zǐ Yǐ Mǎo Zhēng Yuè Èr Shí Rì Yè Jì Mèng
Su Shi · Song · Ci lyric

Ten years — the living and the dead, apart, both lost from sight.

I do not try to think of you,

Yet can't forget you, day or night.

Your lonely grave lies a thousand li away;

I have no place to tell my grief, my plight.

Even if we met, you would not know me now —

My face all dust,

My temples frost-white.

Last night in a dream I suddenly came home:

By the little window there,

You sat, combing your hair.

We looked at each other and could not speak —

Only a thousand lines of tears.

I know the place that breaks my heart, year after year:

The moonlit night,

The hill of short pines, there.

About this poem

Written in 1075 on the tenth anniversary of the death of Su Shi's first wife, Wang Fu, who died at twenty-six. The first great elegy in the ci form, and perhaps the most moving in Chinese. In the dream she is simply doing her hair by the window, as she did every morning; neither of them can speak. Su Shi had planted pine saplings on her grave.
