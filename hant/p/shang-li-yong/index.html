上李邕

〔唐〕李白

大鵬一日同風起，扶搖直上九萬里。

假令風歇時下來，猶能簸卻滄溟水。

世人見我恆殊調，聞餘大言皆冷笑。

宣父猶能畏後生，丈夫未可輕年少。

英译 English translation

To Li Yong

Shàng Lǐ Yōng
Li Bai · Tang · Seven-character old-style verse

The great Peng bird, one day, will rise up with the wind,

And spiral straight ninety thousand li into the sky;

And even if the wind should drop, and it come down,

It still could toss the waters of the vast sea high.

The people of the world find all my tunes odd,

And when they hear my boasts they sneer and scoff;

But Confucius said the young deserve respect —

A real man shouldn't brush a young man off.

About this poem

Written by the young Li Bai to Li Yong, a famous senior official who had treated him dismissively. The Peng is the colossal bird in Zhuangzi that rises ninety thousand li on a whirlwind. Confucius had said "the young are to be held in awe" — so Li Bai turns the sage's words against his elder.
