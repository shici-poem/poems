塞下曲（其三）

〔唐〕盧綸

月黑雁飛高，單于夜遁逃。

欲將輕騎逐，大雪滿弓刀。

英译 English translation

Frontier Song (III)

Sài Xià Qǔ Qí Sān
Lu Lun · Tang · Five-character quatrain

No moon; the wild geese fly up high and far;

The khan has fled, escaping in the night.

We'd chase him down with our light horse —

Heavy snow covers bows and blades in white.

About this poem

The night is so dark that the khan's escape is known only from the startled geese. The last line — the cavalry poised, snow piling on their weapons — freezes the moment before the chase.
