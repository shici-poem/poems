垓下歌

〔秦〕項羽

力拔山兮氣蓋世。時不利兮騅不逝。

騅不逝兮可奈何！虞兮虞兮奈若何！

英译 English translation

Song of Gaixia

Gāi Xià Gē
Xiang Yu · Qin · Mixed-meter verse

My strength could tear up mountains; my spirit overtopped the world;

But the times are against me, and my dappled steed won't go.

My steed won't go — what can be done?

Yu, my Yu — what will become of you now?

About this poem

In 202 BC, surrounded at Gaixia by Liu Bang's armies and hearing the songs of his own homeland, Chu, sung in the enemy camp (四面楚歌, "Chu songs on all sides", now the idiom for being besieged), Xiang Yu drank with his beloved consort Yu and sang this song. She is said to have killed herself; he broke out, fought his last battle and died at the Wu River. The scene is the subject of the opera Farewell My Concubine.
