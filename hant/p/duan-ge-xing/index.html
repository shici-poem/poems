短歌行

〔魏晉〕曹操

對酒當歌，人生幾何！

譬如朝露，去日苦多。

慨當以慷，憂思難忘。

何以解憂？唯有杜康。

青青子衿，悠悠我心。

但為君故，沉吟至今。

呦呦鹿鳴，食野之苹。

我有嘉賓，鼓瑟吹笙。

明明如月，何時可掇？

憂從中來，不可斷絕。

越陌度阡，枉用相存。

契闊談讌，心念舊恩。

月明星稀，烏鵲南飛。

繞樹三匝，何枝可依？

山不厭高，海不厭深。

周公吐哺，天下歸心。

英译 English translation

A Short Song

Duǎn Gē Xíng
Cao Cao · Wei & Jin · Four-character verse

Here's wine — let's sing!

How long does a man's life last?

It's like the morning dew:

So many days already past.

I'm stirred, I'm fierce, I'm moved;

My cares will not depart.

What can dispel my sorrow?

Only Du Kang's wine can soothe the heart.

"Blue, blue, your collar;

Long, long, my longing's flow" —

It's only for your sake

I've pondered, long and low.

"Yo-yo," the deer are calling,

Grazing the wild duckweed —

I have fine guests here with me:

Play zithers, blow the reed!

Bright, bright, like the moon —

When can it be in my grasp?

My sorrow wells from deep within,

And no hand can unclasp.

You've crossed the paths through fields

To come and visit me;

After so long, we feast and talk,

Recalling old amity.

The moon is bright, the stars are few;

The crows and magpies southward fly;

They circle the tree three times —

Where's a branch where they can lie?

The mountain never minds how high,

The sea how deep it grows;

The Duke of Zhou spat out his meal —

And all the world's heart to him goes.

About this poem

Cao Cao's banquet song, written around 208. It begins with the brevity of life and with wine (Du Kang was the legendary first brewer), then quotes two Book of Songs poems to express his longing for men of talent to join him. The crows and magpies circling a tree, finding no branch, are able men without a lord; the Duke of Zhou, who would spit out his food three times in a meal to receive a visitor, is Cao Cao's model — and so the whole world will give him its heart. In Romance of the Three Kingdoms he sings it on the eve of Red Cliff.
