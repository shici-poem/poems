長歌行

〔漢〕漢樂府

青青園中葵，朝露待日晞。

陽春佈德澤，萬物生光輝。

常恐秋節至，焜黃華葉衰。

百川東到海，何時復西歸？

少壯不努力，老大徒傷悲！

英译 English translation

A Long Song

Cháng Gē Xíng
Han Yuefu Ballads · Han · Yuefu ballad

In the garden, the mallows grow green;

The morning dew waits for the sun to dry.

Bright spring spreads its blessings wide,

And all things shine beneath the sky.

But I fear the autumn season's coming,

When flowers and leaves turn yellow and die.

A hundred rivers flow east to the sea —

When will they ever flow back to the west?

If you don't strive while young and strong,

You'll grieve in vain when old, with no rest.

About this poem

An anonymous Han ballad. 少壮不努力，老大徒伤悲 — "if you don't work hard when young, you'll grieve in vain when old" — is one of the most quoted proverbs in Chinese, painted on school walls everywhere.
