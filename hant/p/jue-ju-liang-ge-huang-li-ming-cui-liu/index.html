絕句（兩個黃鸝鳴翠柳）

〔唐〕杜甫

兩個黃鸝鳴翠柳，一行白鷺上青天。

窗含西嶺千秋雪，門泊東吳萬里船。

英译 English translation

Quatrain (Two Golden Orioles)

Jué Jù Liǎng Gè Huáng Lí Míng Cuì Liǔ
Du Fu · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

Two golden orioles sing in the emerald willow;

A line of white egrets climbs the blue of the sky.

My window frames the West Range's thousand-autumn snow;

At my gate, boats from Eastern Wu, ten thousand li, lie by.

About this poem

Four lines, four perfectly parallel images — yellow and green, white and blue; near and far; sound and stillness — like four panels of a painted screen. Written at his Chengdu cottage in 764 when peace had briefly returned: the boats from Wu, on the lower Yangtze, mean the river route east is open again.

Earlier translations 旧译

“Thoughts of Home”W. J. B. Fletcher, 1919

Amid the jade-green willow trees two golden orioles sing.

Across the clear blue sky a flight of soaring egrets wing.

The sighs of chilly Autumn, that breathe eternal snow

From Ormei's lofty mountain, about the casement flow.

Ah! would that they could take me back the thousand miles and more

From hence to home—those goodly ships that anchor at my door!

Translated by W. J. B. Fletcher, from Gems of Chinese Verse (1919) · public domain.

“A Landscape”Herbert A. Giles, 1926

Two orioles sit in the green willows singing;

See egrets in flight to the blue sky are winging!

From my window the snow-peaks eternal I spy,

And an ocean-bound vessel is anchored hard by.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Gems of Chinese Literature: Verse, 2nd ed. (1926) · public domain.
