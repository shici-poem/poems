蘇幕遮·懷舊

〔宋〕范仲淹

碧雲天，黃葉地，秋色連波，波上寒煙翠。

山映斜陽天接水，芳草無情，更在斜陽外。

黯鄉魂，追旅思，夜夜除非，好夢留人睡。

明月樓高休獨倚，酒入愁腸，化作相思淚。

英译 English translation

Su Mu Zhe: Remembering

Sū Mù Zhē Huái Jiù
Fan Zhongyan · Song · Ci lyric

Blue clouds above, yellow leaves below;

The autumn colours meet the waves,

And on the waves, cold mist of jade-green glow.

Hills catch the setting sun; the sky meets the water;

The heartless fragrant grass

Stretches on beyond the sunset's glow.

My homesick soul is dark;

My traveller's thoughts pursue —

Night after night, unless

Sweet dreams can keep me sleeping through.

In bright moonlight, don't lean on the high tower alone:

The wine goes down into a grieving heart,

And turns to tears of longing, too.

About this poem

A rare tender lyric by a stern statesman. 碧云天，黄叶地 — "blue clouds in the sky, yellow leaves on the ground" — was borrowed by Wang Shifu for The Romance of the Western Chamber. The fragrant grass stretching beyond the sunset is the road home, endlessly far; and the famous last line: wine poured into a sad heart turns to tears of longing.
