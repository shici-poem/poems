浣溪沙（一曲新詞酒一杯）

〔宋〕晏殊

一曲新詞酒一杯，去年天氣舊亭臺。夕陽西下幾時回？

無可奈何花落去，似曾相識燕歸來。小園香徑獨徘徊。

英译 English translation

Washing Silk in the Stream (A New Song, a Cup of Wine)

Huàn Xī Shā Yī Qǔ Xīn Cí Jiǔ Yī Bēi
Yan Shu · Song · Ci lyric

A new song, and a cup of wine;

The weather's just like last year's, the same old pavilion, too.

The sun sets in the west — when will it come back in time?

Nothing can be done: the flowers fall away;

As if we'd met before, the swallows come back home.

Along the fragrant garden path, alone I pace and roam.

About this poem

The most famous couplet of the Northern Song: 无可奈何花落去，似曾相识燕归来 — "nothing can be done, the flowers fall; as if old friends, the swallows return." Loss and return, the same and not the same: time's cycle in two lines. Yan Shu is said to have had the second line for years before he found the first.
