清平樂（別來春半）

〔五代〕李煜

別來春半，觸目柔腸斷。

砌下落梅如雪亂，拂了一身還滿。

雁來音信無憑，路遙歸夢難成。

離恨恰如春草，更行更遠還生。

英译 English translation

Pure Serene Music (Since We Parted)

Qīng Píng Yuè Bié Lái Chūn Bàn
Li Yu · Five Dynasties · Ci lyric

Since we parted, half of spring is gone;

All I see breaks my heart in two.

Fallen plum blossoms swirl like snow below the steps;

I brush them off — again I'm covered through.

The geese come back, but bring no word from you;

The road's too long for dreams to bring me home.

The sorrow of parting is just like spring grass:

The farther you go, the thicker it has grown.

About this poem

Probably written when Li Yu's brother was held as a hostage at the Song court. The last couplet — parting's grief like spring grass, growing thicker the farther one travels — is one of the most admired images in the ci.
