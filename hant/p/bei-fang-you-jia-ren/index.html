北方有佳人

〔漢〕李延年

北方有佳人，絕世而獨立。

一顧傾人城，再顧傾人國。

寧不知傾城與傾國，佳人難再得。

英译 English translation

In the North There Is a Beauty

Běi Fāng Yǒu Jiā Rén
Li Yannian · Han · Yuefu ballad

In the north there lives a lovely maid,

Peerless in all the world, she stands alone.

One glance from her, and a city falls;

A second glance, and a kingdom's overthrown.

Who would not risk a city or a kingdom?

A beauty like her is not twice known.

About this poem

Sung by Li Yannian, a musician at the court of Emperor Wu of Han around 100 BC — about his own sister. The emperor asked whether such a woman existed; she was brought to him and became his beloved Lady Li. 倾城倾国, "toppling cities and states", became the stock phrase for supreme beauty.
