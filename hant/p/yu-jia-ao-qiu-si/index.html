漁家傲·秋思

〔宋〕范仲淹

塞下秋來風景異，衡陽雁去無留意。

四面邊聲連角起，千嶂裡，長煙落日孤城閉。

濁酒一杯家萬里，燕然未勒歸無計。

羌管悠悠霜滿地，人不寐，將軍白髮征夫淚。

英译 English translation

The Fisherman's Pride: Autumn Thoughts

Yú Jiā Ào Qiū Sī
Fan Zhongyan · Song · Ci lyric

When autumn comes to the frontier, the land looks strange;

The geese fly off to Hengyang, with no wish to remain.

On every side the frontier's sounds rise with the bugles' call;

Among a thousand peaks,

Long smoke, the setting sun, a lonely fort's gates shut again.

A cup of cloudy wine, and home ten thousand li away;

No victory carved on Mount Yanran — so no way home.

The Qiang flutes wail and wail; frost covers all the ground;

No one sleeps:

The general's hair is white; the soldiers weep alone.

About this poem

Fan Zhongyan commanded the north-western frontier against the Western Xia from 1040. Unlike most frontier poems, written by poets who imagined war, this is a general's own lyric — bleak and honest. Geese fly south as far as Hengyang, where they were said to turn back; on Mount Yanran a Han general carved a record of victory, which the Song has not achieved. The last line gives tears to general and soldier alike.
