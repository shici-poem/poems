古朗月行（節選）

〔唐〕李白

小時不識月，呼作白玉盤。

又疑瑤臺鏡，飛在青雲端。

英译 English translation

Song of the Bright Moon (excerpt)

Gǔ Lǎng Yuè Xíng Jié Xuǎn
Li Bai · Tang · Yuefu ballad

When I was small, I didn't know the moon —

I called it a white jade plate up in the sky,

Or thought it was the Jasper Terrace mirror,

Flying above the blue clouds, way up high.

About this poem

The opening of a longer poem, recited by every Chinese child: a little boy's guesses at what the moon must be. The Jasper Terrace (瑶台) is the dwelling of immortals, so the moon is their mirror.

Earlier translations 旧译

“Boyhood Fancies”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

In days gone by the moon appeared to my still boyish eyes

Some bright jade plate or mirror from the palace of the skies.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
