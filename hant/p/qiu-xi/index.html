秋夕

〔唐〕杜牧

銀燭秋光冷畫屏，輕羅小扇撲流螢。

天階夜色涼如水，臥看牽牛織女星。

英译 English translation

Autumn Evening

Qiū Xī
Du Mu · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

A silver candle's autumn glow chills the painted screen;

With a little silk fan she chases fireflies as they fly.

On the palace steps the night is cool as water's sheen;

She lies and gazes at the Herdboy and the Weaver in the sky.

About this poem

A lonely palace lady on an autumn night. The silk fan was a symbol of a discarded woman, put away when summer ends; fireflies were said to breed in neglected grass. She watches the two star-lovers who meet once a year — more than she can hope for.

Earlier translations 旧译

“Lovers Parted”Herbert A. Giles, 1898

Across the screen the autumn moon stares coldly from the sky;

With silken fan I sit and flick the fireflies sailing by.

The night grows colder every hour,—it chills me to the heart

To watch the Spinning Maiden from the Herdboy far apart.

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, from Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898) · public domain.
