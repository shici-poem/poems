如夢令（常記溪亭日暮）

〔宋〕李清照

常記溪亭日暮，沉醉不知歸路。

興盡晚回舟，誤入藕花深處。

爭渡，爭渡，驚起一灘鷗鷺。

英译 English translation

As in a Dream (I Often Recall the Stream Pavilion)

Rú Mèng Lìng Cháng Jì Xī Tíng Rì Mù
Li Qingzhao · Song · Ci lyric

I often recall the stream pavilion at close of day:

So drunk, we never knew the way back home.

Our fun all spent, we turned the boat back late,

And drifted deep among the lotus, lost to roam.

"Row hard! Row hard!" —

And up flew a whole shoal of gulls and egrets, white as foam.

About this poem

A happy memory of girlhood in Jinan: tipsy after a day out, the girls row home in the dusk, lose their way among the lotus, and splash their way out, sending up a flock of startled birds. 争渡 can mean "row hard!" or "how do we get through?" — both fit the giggling panic.
