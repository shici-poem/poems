元日

〔宋〕王安石

爆竹聲中一歲除，春風送暖入屠蘇。

千門萬戶曈曈日，總把新桃換舊符。

英译 English translation

New Year's Day

Yuán Rì
Wang Anshi · Song · Seven-character quatrain

Amid the firecrackers, the old year's chased away;

The spring wind brings its warmth into the New Year wine.

On a thousand gates, ten thousand homes, the sun shines on the day

They take the old peach charms down, and put up new ones, fine.

About this poem

Firecrackers drove away evil spirits; tusu wine was drunk for health; peach-wood charms bearing door gods were hung on gates — the ancestors of today's red New Year couplets. Written in 1069 as Wang Anshi launched his great reforms, replacing old charms with new became a symbol of renewal.
