鄉村四月

〔宋〕翁卷

綠遍山原白滿川，子規聲裡雨如煙。

鄉村四月閒人少，才了蠶桑又插田。

英译 English translation

Village in the Fourth Month

Xiāng Cūn Sì Yuè
Weng Juan · Song · Seven-character quatrain

Green covers hills and plains; the streams are full and white;

The cuckoo calls; the rain falls fine as mist.

In the village, the fourth month leaves no one idle in sight —

The silkworms done, the rice is planted, and nothing's missed.

About this poem

Early summer in the Zhejiang countryside: flooded paddies white with sky, green hills, the cuckoo — whose call was heard as "plant the rice!" — and a village where no one has a moment to spare.
