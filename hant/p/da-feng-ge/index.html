大風歌

〔漢〕劉邦

大風起兮雲飛揚。

威加海內兮歸故鄉。

安得猛士兮守四方！

英译 English translation

Song of the Great Wind

Dà Fēng Gē
Liu Bang · Han · Mixed-meter verse

A great wind rises; the clouds fly far and wide.

My power fills the world; I come back home with pride.

Where shall I find brave men to guard the land on every side?

About this poem

In 195 BC, returning as emperor to his home town of Pei after crushing a rebellion, Liu Bang feasted his old neighbours, struck a zither, and sang this song while 120 local children joined in; then he danced, and wept. Triumph and anxiety in three lines: he has won the empire — but who will hold it? He died a few months later.
