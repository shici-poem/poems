梅花（王安石）

〔宋〕王安石

牆角數枝梅，凌寒獨自開。

遙知不是雪，為有暗香來。

英译 English translation

Plum Blossom

Méi Huā Wáng Ān Shí
Wang Anshi · Song · Five-character quatrain

In the corner by the wall, a few plum sprays

Have bloomed alone, defying the bitter cold.

From far away I know it isn't snow —

A hidden fragrance on the air has told.

About this poem

Written in retirement after his reforms had failed. White plum blossom looks like snow — but snow has no scent. Like the plum, the person of integrity stands alone in hard times, and is known by a "hidden fragrance".
