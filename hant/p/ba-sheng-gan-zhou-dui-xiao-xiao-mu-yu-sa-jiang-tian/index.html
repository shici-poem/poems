八聲甘州（對瀟瀟暮雨灑江天）

〔宋〕柳永

對瀟瀟、暮雨灑江天，一番洗清秋。

漸霜風悽緊，關河冷落，殘照當樓。

是處紅衰翠減，苒苒物華休。

惟有長江水，無語東流。

不忍登高臨遠，望故鄉渺邈，歸思難收。

嘆年來蹤跡，何事苦淹留？

想佳人、妝樓顒望，誤幾回、天際識歸舟。

爭知我、倚闌干處，正恁凝愁！

英译 English translation

Eight Beats of Ganzhou (Facing the Evening Rain)

Bā Shēng Gān Zhōu Duì Xiāo Xiāo Mù Yǔ Sǎ Jiāng Tiān
Liu Yong · Song · Ci lyric

Facing the soughing evening rain that sweeps the river and the sky,

Washing autumn clean once more —

Little by little the frosty wind grows harsh and cold,

The passes and the rivers bleak,

The sunset's last glow on the tower's floor.

Everywhere the reds are fading and the greens grow thin;

Slowly, all the splendour of the world is done.

Only the water of the Long River

Flows silently east, on and on.

I cannot bear to climb up high and gaze afar,

Toward my home, so far beyond my sight;

My longing to go home is hard to hold.

I sigh at all my wandering these years —

Why do I linger here, in such a plight?

I picture my love, gazing from her dressing tower,

How many times mistaking, at the sky's edge, some boat for mine;

How could she know that I, here leaning on the rail,

Am frozen in such sorrow, too, tonight?

About this poem

Su Shi, no admirer of Liu Yong's sentimental songs, said of the lines 渐霜风凄紧，关河冷落，残照当楼 that they were "no less than the heights of the Tang". The second stanza imagines his beloved mistaking every boat on the horizon for his — an echo of Wen Tingyun's "a thousand sails go by, and none is his".
