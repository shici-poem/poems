池上

〔唐〕白居易

小娃撐小艇，偷採白蓮回。

不解藏蹤跡，浮萍一道開。

英译 English translation

On the Pond

Chí Shàng
Bai Juyi · Tang · Five-character quatrain

A little child poles a little boat,

Sneaking home with white lotus she has picked.

She hasn't learned to hide her tracks —

Through the duckweed, a clear path has been flicked.

About this poem

A comic snapshot: the child thinks the theft is secret, but the open lane through the floating duckweed gives her away.

Earlier translations 旧译

“The Pond”W. J. B. Fletcher, 1919

Her shallop small the little maiden rows.

With stealthy hand the waterlilies white

She comes to pluck and bear away; nor knows

How to conceal her traces.—See, there goes

Her track across the floating duckweed light.

Translated by W. J. B. Fletcher, from Gems of Chinese Verse (1919) · public domain.
