十一月四日風雨大作（其二）

〔宋〕陸游

僵臥孤村不自哀，尚思為國戍輪臺。

夜闌臥聽風吹雨，鐵馬冰河入夢來。

英译 English translation

A Great Storm on the Fourth Day of the Eleventh Month (II)

Shí Yī Yuè Sì Rì Fēng Yǔ Dà Zuò Qí Èr
Lu You · Song · Seven-character quatrain

Stiff in my bed in a lonely village, I don't pity my plight;

I still long to guard Luntai for my country's sake.

Late at night I lie and hear the wind lash the rain —

And armoured horses on frozen rivers gallop through my dreams till I wake.

About this poem

Written in 1192, when Lu You was sixty-seven and living in retirement. The winter storm outside becomes, in his dream, cavalry charging across frozen rivers on the northern frontier — the war he was never allowed to fight. Luntai was a Tang frontier fort in Central Asia.
