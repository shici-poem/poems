宿駱氏亭寄懷崔雍崔袞

〔唐〕李商隱

竹塢無塵水檻清，相思迢遞隔重城。

秋陰不散霜飛晚，留得枯荷聽雨聲。

英译 English translation

Staying at the Luo Pavilion, Thinking of Cui Yong and Cui Gun

Sù Luò Shì Tíng Jì Huái Cuī Yōng Cuī Gǔn
Li Shangyin · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

The bamboo dock is free of dust, the railings by the water clear;

My longing reaches far to you, past city walls on walls.

The autumn clouds won't lift, and frost is late this year —

The withered lotus leaves are left to hear the rain that falls.

About this poem

Staying at a friend's pavilion, Li Shangyin thinks of two cousins far away. The last line — keep the withered lotus to listen to the rain — is among the most loved lines in Chinese: even dead lotus leaves have their beauty, as an instrument for the sound of rain. In Dream of the Red Chamber, Lin Daiyu says it is the one line of Li Shangyin's she likes.
