正宮·端正好（碧雲天）

〔元〕王實甫

碧雲天，黃花地，西風緊，北雁南飛。

曉來誰染霜林醉？總是離人淚。

英译 English translation

Duanzheng Hao (Blue Clouds Above) — from The Western Chamber

Zhèng Gōng Duān Zhèng Hǎo Bì Yún Tiān
Wang Shifu · Yuan · Sanqu song

Blue clouds above, yellow flowers on the ground,

The west wind blowing hard, the wild geese flying south.

Who dyed the frosted woods so drunken-red at dawn?

It's all the tears of those who part, I've found.

About this poem

From The Romance of the Western Chamber: Cui Yingying sees off her lover Zhang Junrui as he leaves for the capital examinations. It borrows Fan Zhongyan's "blue clouds in the sky, yellow leaves on the ground", and its conceit — that the red autumn maples were dyed by the lovers' tears — is the most famous line in Chinese drama.
