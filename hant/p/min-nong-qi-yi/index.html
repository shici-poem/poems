憫農（其一）

〔唐〕李紳

春種一粒粟，秋收萬顆子。

四海無閒田，農夫猶餓死。

英译 English translation

Pity the Farmers (I)

Mǐn Nóng Qí Yī
Li Shen · Tang · Five-character old-style verse

In spring they sow a single grain;

In autumn reap ten thousand more.

No field lies idle anywhere —

Yet still the farmers starve, as before.

About this poem

Every Chinese child learns these two poems. The first asks a terrible question: with so much harvested, why do the people who grow it starve? (The answer, left unsaid, is rents and taxes.)
