秋思（張籍）

〔唐〕張籍

洛陽城裡見秋風，欲作家書意萬重。

復恐匆匆說不盡，行人臨發又開封。

英译 English translation

Autumn Thoughts (Zhang Ji)

Qiū Sī Zhāng Jí
Zhang Ji · Tang · Seven-character quatrain

In Luoyang town I feel the autumn wind blow;

I sit to write home — ten thousand thoughts to tell.

Afraid I've left out something in my haste, and so,

As the courier's leaving, I open it up again as well.

About this poem

A tiny gesture says everything: the letter is sealed, the messenger is ready to go — and the homesick writer tears it open once more. The autumn wind recalls the Jin official Zhang Han, who saw the autumn wind, thought of the perch of his home river, resigned his post and went home.
